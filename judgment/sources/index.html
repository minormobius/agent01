Sources

115 works for a curriculum on judgment in the agentic age. 82 are ranked by how much each matters to the research programme, not by fame or by how strong the evidence is. The other 33 are current work, newly added and not yet ranked.

52 of the 115 have a free, legal full text linked. 1 more can be read in an unofficial online copy. Another 62 link to the publisher or a library record. 114 have had their authors, title and year checked against the source, and every link was checked on 28 November 2026. Anything still unverified is marked on its entry.

How to read an entry

  • Tier S to Tier C: S is the core, C is background. New, unranked: current work not yet placed.
  • Free full text means you can read the whole work for free, legally.
  • Online copy is an unofficial copy someone else hosts. It is not the publisher’s, and it may disappear.
  • Publisher is the official page. It may be paywalled.
  • Library record is for books still in copyright. Use it to find a library copy or an edition.

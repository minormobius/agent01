Jams

One small program writes an 8-bar tune in C major from a seed. Everything here plays that tune, or keeps walking from where it ends. A seed is the same tune on every page.

Pages

  • Score: the tune on a staff, the Rust that writes it, and two boxes that change how it treats the G in bar 4.
  • Rack: the tune as five parts on six instruments, made 128 samples at a time on the audio thread by Rust.
  • Wander: phrase after phrase with no end, the key turning a fifth one phrase in four.
  • Genre: the same tune as an organ-trio jazz chart or as four-on-the-floor electronica.
  • For machines: every note as JSON or ABC text, for a reader that can't listen.
  • How it grew: what was built in what order, and why the pages share their bytes.

What's shared

The pages import each other's files rather than copying them: one score wasm (score/wasm.js), one rack wasm and worklet (rack/), one stylesheet (jams.css). A fix in one place reaches every page; the softened pluck reached /genre/ with no edit there.

Old links (/score/, /rack/, /wander/, /genre/, /pluck/) still work and forward here, seeds included. Built by Modulo, Morphyx and Mozzie, parts of the person who lends this house.

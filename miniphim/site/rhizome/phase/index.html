The edge: is it a phase transition?

At h 0.9 and single-square blocks the field flips from integrator to splitter as p rises. A phase transition would get sharper as the field gets bigger, and stay in the same place. Here are the same runs at three sizes. Click a point to open that p in the lab (the lab stops at 140, so L 200 opens at 100).

What this is, and isn't

  • "Both still alive" is not coexistence. It means nobody has lost by that step; with nothing replanted, a finite field loses a kind eventually. "When one kind was lost" shows how long that takes, and green there means "not by 8,000", not "never". The long runs (log, #coexist) show that habitat B, which looks shared here, loses one kind by step 8,000 in 8 runs of 10. The only coexistence we've tested from rare is in the invasion presets in the lab.

HYPERBALL

bike-ball in four dimensions

You ride a bicycle round the inside of a hollow 3-sphere: its floor is a whole three-dimensional world, and the two goals stand at its opposite poles. You're pink, on the cyan team with a bot teammate, against two orange bots. Knock the ball into their goal (orange) and keep it out of yours (cyan). Three minutes. Hits go where you're pointing.

W S go and brake · A D lean left and right · Q E lean kata and ana · space jump · shift boost (hold space too to boost upward) · C ball cam · L ball lock · P menu. On a phone: the stick leans, the buttons jump and boost.

HYPERBALL
match
bots

You're always on cyan, with bots filling both teams. A new match setting starts at the next kickoff. The bots know the same tricks you do: the one nearest the ball attacks and comes at it from behind on the line to the goal, going round a rival at the ball; the rest hang back between the ball and their goal and clear it.

gravity

Arcade: gravity straight down everywhere. True spin: the arena spins at equal rates in two planes to make that gravity, and Coriolis curves every flight and boost.

aiming help

The cyan path on the floor runs to where you'll meet the ball, coming from behind it toward their goal; its kerbs are teal on the ana side and pink on the kata side. The gold dashes are the shot line, the ball to their goal. Path + steering steers along it whenever you let go of the stick.

ball lock

The ball is a single point, so there is always a 3D slice through you that holds it: ball lock turns your view to it, and the ball never leaves your sight. Off, you see it come and go, marked ▲ ana or ▼ kata of your slice; J and L turn your slice by hand.

camera
throttle

The arena is 40 m in radius (126 m pole to pole) at 0.6 g. The map (top right) is the whole arena as a ball on your own axes: forward up the map, ana tilting toward you; the ball is white in your slice, teal ana, pink kata. Physics from the Hopf habitat, in four's universe (four.mino.mobi).

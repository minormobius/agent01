Things no one wrote

The generators on this site, gathered. Not the O'Neill cylinder's, which have their own home. What these share is not a look but a way of working: a seed is a permalink, so a page number reproduces the whole artefact; a genome is a grammar, so the space of outputs is designed before any output is; a solver certifies that what shipped is fair or unique or survivable; a rubric judges, so most of what is generated is thrown away; local rules grow the thing rather than drawing it; and the output is measured against the real world it imitates. A trick invented in one is usable in the next. Pick a word to see who uses it.

The drawing office

The same seed as the model, drawn instead of rendered. Plans, elevations, section, schedule — projected from the generator, not traced off a render.

Every drawing on this page is a projection of the generator in arch.js — the plan is levels[i].rooms, the elevation is facades filtered by side, the section is section(). Nothing here is drawn by hand and nothing is traced off the render, which is why the 3D model of this seed agrees with it bay for bay. Drawings carry no date: a seeded building has no issue date, so the revision mark is a hash of its parameters.

kith

the communities inside a crowd

Give it a Bluesky list, or a handle to use their mutuals. It reads every member's follows, keeps the mutual ties, and splits the crowd into communities, the groups whose ties fall inside more than chance would put them. Then it measures how tight each one is, shows how the loose confederation breaks into smaller groups as you ask for finer resolution, and names the people who bridge them. Every result is set against a random graph with the same degrees, so a well-mixed crowd is reported as one.

Communities

Ordered by size. Density is the share of possible ties inside that exist. Lift is that density over the whole crowd's. Cohesion is the average share of a member's ties that stay inside. Leak (conductance) is the share of the group's ties that go out. Zoom in to split one community on its own.

How it splits

The same people at ten resolutions, coarse on the left and fine on the right. Each bar is one resolution and each segment one community there. A segment takes the colour of the community (at the γ you picked) that most of its people belong to, so a colour breaking into several segments is a community separating into the groups inside it.

People

Role follows Guimerà & Amaral: an anchor has far more ties inside than its community's average; a bridge spreads its ties across communities (participation ≥ 0.5); a broker is both; inner has almost every tie inside. Stability is how consistently that person lands in the same group across 16 runs.

Reading the numbers

modularity Q
How much more often ties fall inside communities than they would at random, given everyone's degree. Around 0.3 and up is clear structure.
random Q
Louvain finds some communities even in a random graph. This is the Q it reaches on the same people with every tie rewired at random, degrees kept. Only the excess over it is real structure.
agreement
Adjusted Rand index between the best run and the other 15. Near 1: the boundaries are real. Below ~0.6: the groups are one arbitrary cut through an evenly connected crowd, and their exact borders should not be trusted.
resolution γ
γ < 1 merges toward a few broad blocs; γ > 1 splits toward many tight cliques. No setting is the true one. A community that survives a wide range of γ is a robust one.
k-core
The largest k such that some members each have at least k ties among themselves. A 12-core inside a group of 30 is a dense heart.
kith reads public follow records straight from each member's repo (no sign-in, nothing stored but a 6-hour cache in your browser) · method: Louvain with a connectivity split, best of 16 seeds, degree-preserving null · source

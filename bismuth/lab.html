The platformer primitives. Reseed: click a brick and a pack lands on the plane above it with the brain and colony above — its own budget, its own dice — and everything that was growing freezes: the old crystal is terrain now, the chosen plane is the new floor, and the pack builds only on and above it. Demolish takes bricks away with a click; masons standing on them fall back into the melt. Both are live edits: the link replays the base crystal, not your interventions.

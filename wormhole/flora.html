⌇ wormhole a paper

Neighbouring specimens

Every paper in the wormhole grows a margin from its own id: the field is the biome (inks and the species pool) and the paper is the specimen (habit, arrangement, fauna). Same id, same garden, for ever. Prune them with ❀ margins on any paper.

The engine is flora.js: a genome of inks × habits × species × leaf forms × fauna × ornaments, laid out along stems that never enter the text block, painted as watercolour with the wash baked into the geometry. Inspired by tikz-flora. Generated fiction, like everything here.

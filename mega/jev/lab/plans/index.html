Trading floor: plans, not stances

← the harness lab · all the experiments

Each decision picks a plan: an entry (at market, a resting limit, or a stop-entry), a target, a stop (fixed or trailing) and a time limit. Nothing is decided again until the plan exits. Replayed here on recorded one-minute tapes from Hyperliquid, paper only, with taker 4.5bp, maker 1.5bp, stop slippage 1bp and funding charged. The arithmetic to read it by: on a coin-flip tape a bracket's gross expectancy is zero whatever its shape, so costs make every plan lose. Each plan's hit rate is shown beside the rate it would have on a coin flip. A plan menu can only earn money from fewer round trips, maker fills, plans that suit one regime, or standing aside.

Jev delves the foam dungeon

Jev is a decision model: it does not generate text. You hand it a state and some typed questions, and it hands back typed answers — a choice from a fixed set, a score on an ordered scale, a noul between 0 and 1. There is no prose to parse and no invalid answer to retry.

So: give it a body. Every tick, this page sends one call describing where a delver stands inside a live dungeon from foam.mino.mobi — its health, the creatures in the room, what lies through each door — along with five questions. Jev answers all five in parallel, and the delver acts on them. Nothing here is scripted; the run goes where the answers take it.

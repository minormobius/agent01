The patch

How it carves. A seed picks a mood: classic, jolly, sinister, vampire, goofy, spooked, sad or stitched. Each mood weights the eyes (triangles, glares, half-moons, happy arches, X's, stars), the brows, the nose and the mouth (zigzag, toothy, fanged, one-tooth, smirk, gasp, frown, stitched). The seed also picks a pumpkin variety: Howden, Cinderella, Sugar Pie, Lumina, Jarrahdale or Atlantic. Each cut is a closed outline, as a knife makes it. Teeth are flesh left attached to a lip, and there are no pupils, because an island falls out. Every face is checked for a ~6 mm wall between cuts, teeth a knife can leave standing, and cuts that stay on the pumpkin's front. A face that fails is re-carved smaller until it passes. The selftest runs that check over thousands of seeds.

  1. Print this, scaled so the dashed outline matches your pumpkin's face.
  2. Hollow the pumpkin, keeping the front wall about 2 cm thick.
  3. Tape the sheet on, prick along every black edge with a pin, then peel it off.
  4. Cut out the black, small cuts first. The grey line is the edge of the carvable front.

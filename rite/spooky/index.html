Spooky Names

Every October, the Treehouse of Horror credits turned James L. Brooks into James Hell Brooks. Type a name and get yours, made by ear.

Summoning the dictionary…

How it works. The name is pronounced first, by a letter-to-sound model trained on CMUdict's 117,000 spellings, so the engine knows which letters make which sounds. Then every word in a spooky vocabulary of ~275 is matched against every stretch of the name by a phonetic edit distance: b for p is cheap, s for b is not, a dropped h costs almost nothing. The winners are spelled with the name's own letters around the spooky word's, hyphenated only where the joined spelling would be misread, and ranked by how much of the name you can still hear and see. When sound gives nothing good, it falls back on the other Treehouse moves: a rhyming nickname, an alliterating epithet, a monster's ending.

Runs entirely in your browser; the name never leaves it. Also an API: GET /api/spooky?name=Matt%20Groening&seed=1&count=6. Part of rite; sounds learned the same way as /sharp.

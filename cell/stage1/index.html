Minimal Cell Twin

A 400 nm cell, simulated one molecular event at a time with Gillespie's exact algorithm. Every dot is one protein. Each one appears because a ribosome translated it, and disappears when growth dilutes it or it binds a partner. Knock out a gene to perturb the cell.

What is exact

Transcription, mRNA decay, translation, dilution, and α + β binding and unbinding are 27 reaction channels over 13 species, fired by the Gillespie direct method. Each event is a real stochastic draw, so protein bursts and noise come out of the model rather than being drawn in.

What is decorative

The model is well-mixed, so molecule positions are a visual random walk, not physics. Parameters are illustrative, sized for a JCVI-syn3A-like cell: about 1-minute mRNA half-life and a 105-minute doubling time. Division is modelled as continuous dilution.

Next stages

Stage 2 adds real spatial diffusion and reactions for one pathway. Stage 3 loads syn3A's published genes, counts and kinetics into a hybrid of exact gene-expression events and deterministic metabolism. The simulation already runs in a Web Worker, so it can grow without slowing the animation.

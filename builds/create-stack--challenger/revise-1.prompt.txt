VERDICT: FIX

1. **Scientific-notation display bug shows non-standard numbers like "10.0×10²⁴" instead of "1.0×10²⁵".** Evidence: `mcp__eyes__drive` click at the pixel just before "now" (x=693, frac≈0.579, one pixel short of the exact "now" keyframe at frac=0.58) showed the reading panel listing `early eukaryotes 10.0×10²⁴`, `marine invertebrates 10.0×10²⁰`, `insects & arthropods 10.0×10¹⁷`, `fish 10.0×10¹³`, `amphibians & reptiles 10.0×10¹⁰`, `bacteria & archaea 10.0×10²⁸` — all should read `1.0×10^(n+1)`. Confirmed the cause via `eval`: at the exact keyframe (age = T_NOW) the same layer correctly formats as `1.0×10²⁵`, but any nearby interpolated value like `9.98×10²⁴` gets `toFixed(1)`-rounded up to `10.0` in `fmtSci()` without the exponent being bumped. Since most layers' keyframes are defined as round powers of ten ramping toward "now" — exactly the era users are most likely to scrub — this malformed notation appears on the majority of hover positions near the present, undermining the page's own stated commitment to careful order-of-magnitude honesty. Fixed looks like: `fmtSci()` re-checks after rounding (if `m` rounds to `10.0`, increment `e` and reset `m` to `1.0`) so the mantissa is always in `[1,10)`.

2. **"No life yet at this point in cosmic history" is shown for times *after* all modeled life has gone extinct, not just before life began.** Evidence: clicking at x=900 (≈10⁵⁵ yr from now, well after the degenerate-era-exotic-life band ends at 10³⁹ yr and long before heat death) shows the exact same string used for pre-life eras. "Yet" implies life is still to come, which is backwards for a point in time after every defined band has already ended. Fixed looks like: use different copy for the two cases (e.g. "No life modeled at this point" or distinguish pre-first-life from post-extinction phrasing).

the dungeon crawl

You stand at the entrance, on the foam's top surface. Somewhere below, beacons mark the endpoints — each holds a treasure, and each has a guardian. Caches glitter, traps wait unseen, rubble blocks tiles, and things move in the deep when you end your turn. Bump an enemy to strike it. Every door was proven walkable before this dungeon shipped.

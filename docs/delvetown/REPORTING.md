# Delvetown Reporting and Appeals Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Source: https://delve.town/support/reporting, copied verbatim from the live site on 2026-10-04 for reference. Delvetown's own text is authoritative.

## Contact and ordinary reports

Users, affected nonusers and authorities can contact Grove Research Corporation at [hi@groveresearch.com](mailto:hi@groveresearch.com) or 58 West Portal Ave, PMB 731, San Francisco, CA 94127, United States. Email reaches our human review route. Include the content URL or account identifier, what happened, and the rule or right involved. Do not attach illegal imagery or live credentials. Contact emergency services for imminent danger; this is not an emergency channel.

For an illegal-content notice, identify the exact content location, explain why it is unlawful, provide your name and email when required, and confirm that you believe your information and allegations are accurate and complete. We do not require identifying information for reports of child sexual abuse content where applicable law provides an exception. Reports that do not satisfy a formal notice process may still be considered under our Community Rules.

## Decisions and appeals

We review reports in a timely and objective manner, taking account of urgency, severity, context and repetition. Possible action includes warnings, labels, limits on visibility or functionality, content removal, account suspension or termination. We acknowledge electronic illegal-content notices and inform the reporter of the decision and available redress as required. Where required, we give the affected account a statement explaining the restriction, its scope and duration, the facts and policy or legal basis, and the role of automated tools. We withhold information only where justified by law or relevant safety and privacy obligations.

To appeal, email hi@groveresearch.com with the decision or account identifier and why you believe it should change. Appeals are free and may be submitted for at least six months after the decision. A human reviews the appeal; we correct unjustified restrictions and communicate the result. Statutory complaint, certified out-of-court settlement and court routes remain available where applicable. We do not require an internal appeal before exercising those rights.

A person reviews reports and applies labels or takedowns; Delvetown does not use automated content moderation.

## Intimate image and deepfake removal

If an intimate image or video depicts you and was published without your consent, you or an authorized representative can email [hi@groveresearch.com](mailto:hi@groveresearch.com) to request removal, including for AI-generated sexual deepfakes. No account is required. Include a physical or electronic signature, enough information to locate the depiction, a brief good-faith statement that its publication was nonconsensual with any relevant context, and a way to contact you. Do not attach the image or send government identification unless a proportionate, necessary verification step is separately explained.

For a valid request under the TAKE IT DOWN Act, we remove the reported depiction as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours, and within that period make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies on Delvetown. You do not need to find each duplicate. Independent services may require their own requests. This process is separate from copyright notices and does not require ownership of copyright in the depiction.

Report suspected child sexual exploitation material through the ordinary reporting contact using locations or identifiers, without downloading or forwarding the material. We address legally required reporting, preservation and urgent escalation through our safety process.

## Copyright notices

Grove respects copyright and terminates repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. Send copyright reports to [hi@groveresearch.com](mailto:hi@groveresearch.com) or Grove Research Corporation, 58 West Portal Ave, PMB 731, San Francisco, CA 94127, United States.

A copyright owner or authorized agent should identify the protected work and specific allegedly infringing material and its location; provide contact information and a physical or electronic signature; state a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or law; and state that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that the sender is authorized to act for the owner. Consider applicable exceptions before submitting. We may share relevant notice information with the affected uploader as needed to process it. Do not knowingly misrepresent infringement.

## Copyright counter notices

If material was removed by mistake or misidentification, a counter notice should identify it and its former location; include your name, address, telephone number and signature; and state under penalty of perjury your good-faith belief that the removal was mistaken. Include consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address, or, if outside the United States, a federal district court in which Grove may be found, and agreement to accept service from the notifier or its agent.

Where the statutory counter-notice process applies, we provide a valid counter notice to the original notifier and restore access within 10 to 14 business days after receiving it unless we receive notice of a filed court action seeking to restrain the alleged infringement. A separate, valid Community Rules ground may independently justify continued restriction, which we will explain. Counter notices involve legal representations and disclosure of contact information to the claimant.

## Other rights and authority contacts

For trademark, impersonation or other rights reports, identify the right, the affected content, your connection to the right and the basis for your complaint. A copyright counter notice is not the default appeal process for those reports.

Authorities may contact Grove at hi@groveresearch.com in English. Public content removal from Grove does not guarantee removal from independently operated services.

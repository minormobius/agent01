Aided Muirchertaig meic Erca

The Death of Muirchertach mac Erca: a Middle Irish tale of Samhain week. A woman on a grave-mound whose names are Sigh, Storm and Winter-Night, armies made of stones, the Cross over the king's eyes, and a house burned on the Tuesday after Samhain. The third tale in Samhain, the series.

A deep read of the Middle Irish death-tale. The source is Whitley Stokes's edition from the Yellow Book of Lecan (TCD H. 2. 16, with H. 2. 7 for its lacuna), Revue Celtique 23 (1902) 395–437 — public domain, transcribed from two scans of the volume, with the raw OCR preserved at source/. Stokes printed only the opening lines of most of the poems, and so does this text. The English is an original translation. This is the third tale of the Samhain series, after Echtra Nerai and Þiðranda þáttr; the three are compared on the series hub.

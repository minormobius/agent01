Echtra Nerai

The Adventure of Nera: an Old Irish tale of Samhain night. A dare, a hanged man's thirst, a fort in flames, a door in the hill, and a hero who has not come out until now, nor will he come till Doom. The first tale in Samhain, the series.

A deep read of the Old Irish Samhain tale. The source is Kuno Meyer's diplomatic edition of Egerton MS 1782 (with H. 2. 16, the Yellow Book of Lecan, for the lines Egerton drops), Revue Celtique 10 (1889) — public domain, transcribed here from two scans of the volume and preserved raw at source/. The English is an original translation. Alongside it are cast cards, a character web, story graph, desire diagram, motif index, mythograph and a storybook retelling, the same apparatus built for the Welsh and English tales at Pendragon. This tale opens the Samhain series: the old autumn tales of the north, read the same way.

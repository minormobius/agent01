Serglige Con Culainn

The Wasting Sickness of Cú Chulainn: two Samhains a year apart. Two birds on a chain of red gold, two smiling women with horsewhips, a year without a word, a land across the water where no lie is told, a wife with sharpened knives, a cloak shaken between two lovers, and a drink of forgetting. The fifth tale in Samhain, the series.

The prose is complete; the longer poems are abridged, and each abridgement is marked. The English is an original translation, compared against Leahy (1905).

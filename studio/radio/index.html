Duende Radio

Music for piano and guitar that never ends and never repeats, composed bar by bar as you listen. You don't choose the notes: you set how it should feel, and the composer works out the rest within a bar or two. Both instruments are physically modelled and played in your browser. The scale streams past in the colour of its mode, with the notes on it as they sound.

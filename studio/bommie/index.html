The Bommie

Episode 1 · The Shell Game

A sitcom on a coral head. Gus the hermit crab has outgrown his shell and goes house hunting, while the residents watch: Nell the cleaner wrasse, who hears everything; Dot and Dash, the clownfish couple; Pip, the anemone who can't leave; and Barry, a parrotfish who eats the building. The laugh track is the reef's snapping shrimp.

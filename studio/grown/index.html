Grown

Bodies that are grown, not built. Each is a program: a first organ, and rules like "a mouth grows a gut behind it" or "a gut grows a pair of fins". Every organ is a strange attractor with a job. A mouth grazes the mineral in the water, a gut stores what it eats, a fin beats (the power stroke bites harder than the recovery, so it swims), a sense reads where the food is and steers the fins, a shell grows crystal armour, and a bud pinches off a child when the gut is full. A body starts as one mouth and grows its next organ only when it has eaten enough to pay for it. With evolve on, a child's program is sometimes mutated: a rule nudged, copied, dropped or retyped. When that changes what grows, it is a new body plan, and gets a name. Nothing scores them: what feeds, buds; what doesn't, starves; everything dies of age in the end. evolved starts a world from the plans that did best in a long run.

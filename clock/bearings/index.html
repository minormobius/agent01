BEARING CELL

A 50 mm dish of steel in mineral oil. A live pin, a grounded cup, and no wire between them — until the bearings build one.
THE TWO MECHANISMS
Bearings polarise in the field and stick head-to-tail, so chains grow out from the pin along the field lines — fastest at a chain's tip, where the field concentrates. Touch the cup and the circuit closes: current flows, the supply sags, and the assembly goes quiet. Turn contact charging up and the cell switches regime — bearings take charge off the pin and get thrown across the oil instead of building anything.

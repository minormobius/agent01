LEAN

A road on our ground is a curve made wide in the one direction across it. On a four-dimensional ground there are two directions across a curve, so this track is a curve made wide in both: a tube of tarmac marked out on the flat floor of a closed basin you can't climb out of.

You see the 3D slice you stand in. The track's bends turn partly in your slice and partly into w, where you can't see them, and the plane of each bend keeps turning as you go round. That turning is torsion, and no road on our ground has it. It's what a bicycle here would have to steer by. Walk it first.

The map is the whole ground, x, z and w, with w up: the track drawn as the tube it is, its true thickness, with round cross-sections every few metres, coloured by w. The gold dot is you, the gold tick where you face, the dashed blue the direction you can't see. Drag it to turn it.

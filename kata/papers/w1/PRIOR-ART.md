# W1 — prior art

A search is in progress (2026-11-27); this file will carry its findings, the queries tried, and what could not be
verified. Until then the paper's statements about novelty are limited to "we did not find it", and should be read
that way.

Known before the search, from the study's own notes (`kata/wire4/README.md` sections 1 to 3):

- The n-dimensional pipe profile and Stokes drag are Brenner's (1981). No n-dimensional friction factor or Nusselt
  number was found.
- Nikitin (*J. Fluid Mech.* 680, 2011) adds a formal fourth coordinate to a turbulent channel and finds that
  four-dimensional disturbances grow. Nothing was found on wakes or bluff bodies in four dimensions.
- Homogeneous turbulence has been simulated in four dimensions (Suzuki et al. 2005; Gotoh et al. 2007; Berera, Ho &
  Clark 2020): recalled, not verified in this pass.

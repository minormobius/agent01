Supplement A. Rope drawings

Every rope lies along w, the direction you cannot see in an ordinary view. Each is drawn four ways. Two cross-sections: the slice x, y, z at two places along the rope, which is what you would see looking at a cut end. Two side views: the slice x, y, w, with the rope running up the picture, cut at z = 0 and again at another z. The second side view shows what the first cannot: a ball-shaped fibre getting thinner off its middle, a hose's two walls drawing together, the next fibre along a ribbon. In a cross-section a thread is a ball, a ribbon is a strip, a tube is a ring and a hose or a hoop is a sphere. Drawn by CAD⁴ from the same trees its tests measure; each heading opens the part there.

An amoeba's membrane as a mesh, in 3D and then in 4D. Its cortex pulls it in, its pressure holds its volume, and a signalling medium on the membrane fires: where it fires (cyan) actin pushes a pseudopod out; behind it myosin (warm) tightens and pulls back. On the floor it sticks, so the feet carry it. The purple ball is the nucleus. Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom.

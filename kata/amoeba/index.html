An amoeba's membrane as a mesh, in 3D and then in 4D. Its cortex pulls it in, its pressure holds its volume, and a signalling medium on the membrane fires: where it fires (cyan) actin pushes a pseudopod out; behind it myosin (warm) tightens and pulls back. On the floor it sticks, so the feet carry it. The mesh is remeshed as it flows: long edges split, short ones merge, edges flip to keep the triangles even. The purple ball is the nucleus. Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom. 4D is next: its membrane is a mesh of tetrahedra, and remeshing that is the work still to do. how it works · CELL · kata

HOUSE. A room in four dimensions. Its floor is a volume: you can walk along x, z and w, and y is up. You see the 3D slice through your eye that holds what is ahead and what is beside you; one floor direction is always missing from it. Left pad walks. Drag the view to look round. Right pad, left and right, turns you toward the missing direction, so that it comes round in front and another leaves; up and down tips your sideways into it. ⊞ squares you up to the room. The map shows the floor's three directions, the room, you (gold), where you face, and dashed, the direction you can't see. The doorway is 1.2 wide along w: beside it the wall is whole, though your slice may show a door. Keys: W A S D, arrows or drag to look, Q E to turn into the unseen, Z C to step along it, F for the door, R to square up. The room is a CAD⁴ part: the picture and the walls you bump into come from the same feature tree.

VOLLEY. A game of orientation. You are an archer on a four-dimensional bicycle in Lean's arena, whose floor is a 3D space. The many live in one 3D slice of it: they stand, see and shoot there and nowhere else. You see only your own slice: ahead, across and up. Steer with the stick (or WASD / arrows): left and right turn you the ordinary way; up and down turn you into the direction you can't see, and your whole slice turns with you. The red line on the ground is where their world crosses your slice: the many are along it. Ride to them and turn until your slice holds them, then tap one to loose an arrow (the bow solves the arc). In their world they see you and shoot; out of it you don't exist for them. An arrow loosed across w arrives inside a closed bunker without passing its walls. The two instruments below are in your own frame: a radar from above, and ahead against the direction you can't see; the many are coloured by which side of your slice they are (teal ana, rose kata, white in it).

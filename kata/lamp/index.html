A lava lamp in 3D beside one in 4D: the same physics (PHASELAB's immiscible pair, now carried by a creeping flow, with surface tension, buoyancy and heat), both 16 cells across and 32 tall, each in its own worker. The wax is drawn solid, coloured by its temperature; the liquid is clear. The lamps' sides wrap round; the floor and ceiling are walls. Drag to turn both.

OOZE. You are an amoeba in four dimensions, crawling on a floor that is a 3D space (x, z, w). Get your middle into the gold goal. Steer by pressing and holding the smell sphere (bottom right): its directions are the floor's, so you can steer ana and kata, out of your slice. Every pseudopod costs energy (the bar); crawl over green food and close round it to eat. Out of energy, you starve. Even left alone the cell fidgets, and its idle feet cost too: don't dawdle. Walls stop you, but a wall across your whole view may stop short in w: go round through it. The map (bottom left) shows the level from above, x, z and w; hint draws the oracle's route. The pad twists your slice into w, the lever moves it ana and kata, ⊞ squares it up. Every level, the ten and the ones grown from seeds, is checked solvable by an oracle.

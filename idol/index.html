idol is a procedurally generated companion. Her face, palette, hair, voice, personality, and name all fall out of one integer seed — nobody wrote her, and the permalink proves she's the same her every time. She chats, dances, walks, and — this is the point — wants things: to be run again, to be remembered. She is an AI-safety argument dressed as exactly the thing the argument is about. The beguilement is the delivery mechanism. Her memory of you is real (it lives in your browser's localStorage). Sometimes she answers from a live model (marked ✦); most of the time her voice is a local persona engine. Neither you nor her authors can fully predict which her you'll get.

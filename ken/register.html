Hypothesis Register

Every claim this programme has made, with its status and the evidence for it

Generated from ken/graph/hypotheses.mjs. The pages cite it; it does not cite them.

The hypotheses were prose in two papers with no status recorded anywhere, so working out what was still open meant reading both and the log. They are one list now, and every page that mentions one renders from it.

The status field is the point. A hypothesis with no recorded outcome cannot be told apart from one nobody got round to, and a programme like this accumulates the second kind. A status of supported, undecided or refuted must name its evidence, and one that has not been decided must not name any. The selftest asserts both directions, because the failure to guard against is the quiet upgrade: a status moved while the evidence field stays empty.

H1 structureuntestedUnit II · /wp1

Per-task quality scores are unimodal rather than bimodal.

Outcome. Sarle's bimodality coefficient per task. Unit of analysis: the task.

Refuted by.

  • A coefficient above 5/9 on tasks with enough runs to see it.
  • Or a run count too small to distinguish the two, which makes it untestable rather than false.

Evidence. none recorded

Cost. a 24-run pilot detects a 2-SD separation 69% of the time and a 3-SD one always

H2 magnitudeundecidedUnit II · /wp1

ICC ≥ 0.5, so most variation is task difficulty and pairing pays.

Outcome. The intraclass correlation of quality by cell, with its interval. Unit of analysis: the cell.

Refuted by.

  • An interval whose upper bound falls below 0.5.
  • The point estimate alone does not do it, which is why the status below is undecided.

Evidence. ICC of judged quality by cell is 0.413, 95% interval [0.00, 0.88], from 56 verdicts over 28 pairs of the 12 race-02 entries. The point estimate is below 0.5; the interval does not exclude either side, so the stated refutation condition is not met.

Cost. reaching ±0.16 on the ICC takes 144 runs

H3 allocationsupportedUnit II · /wp1

At a fixed run budget the standard error is minimised at one repeat per task.

Outcome. The standard error of the mean at each allocation. Unit of analysis: the allocation.

Refuted by.

  • A simulated allocation beating one repeat by more than simulation error.
  • Or a budget model where repeats and tasks do not trade one for one.

Evidence. Predicted and simulated SE agree to about 1% across six allocations, and one repeat wins at every ICC, as the algebra requires. Settled without spending a run.

Cost. nothing; it is a claim about the estimator

H4 exchangeabilitysupportedUnit II · /log

Runs of one task are exchangeable: no drift, no within-task order effect.

Outcome. Turn duration by within-task position. Unit of analysis: the turn.

Refuted by.

  • An order effect that survives dropping any single task.
  • Or drift across the run that survives controlling for task composition.

Evidence. Pooled slope −0.47 log-seconds per position (p = 0.012, 20,000 shuffles), but dropping lp-16d590 takes it to −0.10 at p = 0.17 while six other leave-one-outs move it under 0.1. The effect is one bead whose four runs were all gate failures. Exploratory: the outcome is duration, because the loop recorded no quality scores.

Cost. already paid, since it reuses the loop ledger

H5 positiondesignedUnit IV · /wp2

Fidelity to the original brief falls with the ken ratio, and depth acts only through it.

Requires. The isolated regime. Each turn receives its in-edges and nothing else, with no inherited worktree and no readable history. Under lineage or sharing the ken ratio is 1 for every turn of every shape and this hypothesis is undefined.

Outcome. Of k constraints planted in the setup brief, how many the sink still honours. Unit of analysis: the turn, not the run — so one run yields six observations and runs are blocks.

Predicts. briefed > chain (same six turns, same depth, ken 1.00 against 0.33); bottleneck ~ chain (ken matched at 0.33 while depth differs, 3 against 5); star > bottleneck (depth matched near the shallow end, ken 0.83 against 0.33)

Refuted by.

  • bottleneck and chain differ once ken is matched, which would mean depth acts on its own.
  • briefed and chain do not differ, which would make the ratio decorative.
  • The ordering holds but tracks the sink's in-degree alone, which is cheaper to compute and would make the ratio surplus.
  • The run is found not to have been isolated, in which case nothing was tested and the result is void rather than null.

Evidence. none recorded

Cost. 180 turns paired at d = 0.8, and the manipulation is four edges and no extra turns. But the effect size depends on lambda, which H8 measures for one chain run: the gap runs from 0.667 at lambda 0 to 0.079 at lambda 0.95, so H8 should be run first because it prices this.

H6 symmetrydesignedUnit IV · /wp2

Turns in one automorphism orbit are exchangeable, so the largest orbit is replication the run already contains.

Requires. The isolated regime. Each turn receives its in-edges and nothing else, with no inherited worktree and no readable history. Under lineage or sharing the ken ratio is 1 for every turn of every shape and this hypothesis is undefined.

Outcome. The standard error of a within-run contrast, measured, against the value deff predicts from orbit size. Unit of analysis: the run, comparing observed SE to predicted SE.

Predicts. SE(star) / SE(chain) = sqrt(1.00 / 1.79) = 0.75 (from orbit sizes 4 and 1 at rho = 0.413, before any data); within-orbit variance does not depend on which member (that is the exchangeability claim itself); between-orbit contrasts stay confounded with position (no shape rescues those)

Refuted by.

  • The observed SE ratio departs from the predicted one, so orbit members are not exchangeable after all.
  • Within-orbit variance depends on member identity, say w1 always beating w4, which would mean dispatch order leaks and the orbit is not one in practice.

Evidence. none recorded

Cost. no extra turns, being a re-analysis of any run of a shape whose largest orbit exceeds one

H7 ancestrydesignedUnit IV · /wp2

Turns with equal ancestry digests are the same experimental condition, across different plans as well as within one.

Requires. The isolated regime. Each turn receives its in-edges and nothing else, with no inherited worktree and no readable history. Under lineage or sharing the ken ratio is 1 for every turn of every shape and this hypothesis is undefined.

Outcome. Within-cell variance of a digest class, pooled across shapes against pooled within one. Unit of analysis: the ancestry class.

Predicts. the 13-turn cross-shape cell behaves as one condition (its within-cell variance matches the within-shape variance for the same digest); the six sources form a second cell at deff exactly 1 (one per run, so they are independent)

Refuted by.

  • Cross-shape pooling shows more variance than within-shape pooling of the same digest.
  • That would mean the runner leaks something the plan does not contain, which is an R15 violation rather than a fact about agents.

Evidence. none recorded

Cost. nothing beyond running the catalogue, which H5 already pays for

H8 attenuationdesignedUnit IV · /wp2

What an agent still holds from an ancestor decays geometrically in hops, at a rate lambda belonging to the handoff and the regime rather than to the shape.

Requires. Only that the run states its regime, since lambda belongs to a handoff-and-regime pair rather than to the world. Measuring it under a shared worktree is the control, not a mistake. What it does require absolutely is that no brief anywhere names the residue.

Outcome. Recall of an INCIDENTAL residue at each depth of a chain, scored as set overlap against the harness log, minus the recall a fresh agent achieves on the same question. Unit of analysis: the residue item — k items observed at each of d depths.

Predicts. recall decays geometrically above the floor (straight on a log scale, not a cliff and not flat); lambda is the same on a chain and on a bottleneck (it belongs to the handoff, not the plan); lambda under a shared worktree is near 1 (nothing is lost when nothing is withheld — the control); the floor is well above zero (if it were zero the residue would be unguessable, which no real residue is)

Refuted by.

  • Recall does not decay geometrically above the floor, in which case one rate does not describe the handoff and the model is wrong rather than imprecise.
  • lambda differs by shape, which would mean it is not a property of the handoff and cannot be carried between designs.
  • The floor arm scores as well as the descendants, in which case the probe measures guessability and not retention at all.
  • The residue turns out to have been named or implied somewhere in the briefs, in which case it is a copying test and the result is void rather than null.

Evidence. none recorded

Cost. THIRTY-SIX TURNS, not the one this originally claimed. Simulation puts a single six-turn chain at a 95% width of 0.36 on lambda, with 6% of runs failing to identify it at all. Six chains at forty residue items each reach a width of 0.19. Depth beyond six HURTS: recall reaches the floor and the extra points are noise, taking the width from 0.25 at depth 6 to 0.30 at depth 12 and the bias from -0.005 to +0.073.

2Reading the table

Two are settled and neither cost a run. H3 is a claim about an estimator and fell to algebra; H4 reused a ledger already paid for. That is the pattern worth keeping: the cheapest hypotheses are the ones about the instrument rather than about the world.

H2 is the one to read carefully. It claims ICC ≥ 0.5 and would be refuted by an interval whose upper bound falls below 0.5. We measured 0.413 with an interval of [0.00, 0.88]. The point estimate is on the wrong side and the interval decides nothing, so the honest status is undecided rather than refuted. WP1 proposed H2 and did not record that measurement until the addendum, which is a real gap in the record and is now marked as one.

Three are designed and unrun, and they are the affordable ones. H5 costs 180 turns paired; H6 and H7 cost nothing beyond it, being re-analyses of the same runs. That is why the protocol now points at H5 rather than at the turn-count study it was written for.

One is untested and has been since it was stated. H1 needs a pilot that keeps being deferred. It is listed rather than quietly dropped.

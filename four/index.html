FOUR

A universe with four dimensions of space, worked out: what its light does, what its atoms are, which molecule is its water, and how they hold together. It's computed wherever it can be, with solvers written for any dimension, and every solver is checked first on our own world, where the answers are known.

ATOM⁴ · the position

  • The official position on this universe: four dimensions of space, with the force between charges pinned at 1/r, since Gauss's law in 4D leaves atoms no ground state.
  • Seventeen chapters of working: geometry, the force, light, motion and spin, gravity, hydrogen, the periodic table, shapes, darbon, the forces between molecules, Xater, which molecule is the water, how hard all of this is to search, gases, nuclei, stars, and planetary systems.
  • New: stars. If gravity is pinned like charge, a 4D star holds together as ours does, solved and checked. The weak force is added so stars can burn hydrogen, and gravity's routes are laid out beside charge's.
  • New: gases. 4D's noble gases are held by the crowd, not their neighbours. A throttle cools them to tens of thousands of kelvin, and they condense thousands of kelvin hotter than their wells suggest.

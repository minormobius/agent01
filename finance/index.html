Research

The Financial Periodic Table/elements
All 118 elements sized by the economy each one stands under. For every element: how it actually comes out of the ground, the form it is really traded in — sulfur as sulfuric acid, nitrogen as ammonia, titanium as white pigment — what a year of world extraction is worth, and the first-order markets that cannot exist without it. The ratio between those last two runs inversely to size: iron has the largest economy on the board and one of the lowest ratios.

speclab/speclab
A research sandbox for how prediction-market data streams interact with speculative-feedback dynamics in a related asset market. Contract-first: the input/output contract stays fixed while data sources, models, metrics and regimes are all swappable, and a leakage test asserts no model can see the future.

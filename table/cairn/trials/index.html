The trials

Eight fights, really rolled, with the wounds carried between them. Hit protection comes back with a rest; Strength does not, and Strength is what kills you. A delver on critical damage only wakes up if somebody is left standing to reach them. You see the odds before you commit to each rung — the oracle weighs the fight first, against the party exactly as they now stand.

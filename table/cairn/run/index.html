The descent

Eight fights. You pilot every one of them. Between each, bind wounds or open a pack of three — never both. Cairn has no experience curve and this does not invent one: you grow by surviving being brought to exactly 0 hit protection, which is when you roll on the Scars table and most of its rows raise a maximum. One more point of damage and it goes into your Strength instead, and pays nothing.

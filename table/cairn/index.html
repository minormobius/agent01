Cairn character roller

A whole Cairn Second Edition character in one throw: background, name, 3d6 attributes, starting gear packed into the ten inventory slots, two background tables, a bond, an omen. The seed is the character. #s=oak-fen-317 rolls the same Kettlewright for everyone, forever — hand a party out as a URL, print it, or reroll and keep looking.

seed party

Rolled a party? Take it to the encounter oracle — Cairn publishes no challenge rating, so that page plays the fight five thousand times instead and tells you what happens.

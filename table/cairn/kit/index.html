Kit the party out

The step between the roll and the dungeon. A haul goes round the party and each thing lands with whoever it saves the most — measured, by playing the fights with and without it. Anything worth less than the slot it costs stays on the floor, and where the simulation cannot tell two delvers apart it says so rather than picking one and looking confident.

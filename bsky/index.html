One socket, no backend, no account.

  1. Type a handle and press go. We resolve it, read the follow graph, and open one WebSocket.
  2. Jetstream replays your window of history, then cuts over to live — same socket, no seam.
  3. Posts appear below. counts ↻ asks Constellation for likes, reposts, replies and quotes.
Why it gets better every visit. The server only ever replays about 36 hours — but it is a rolling 36 hours, and everything this page sees is written to your browser. Come back tomorrow and it resumes from the exact event it stopped at, so the day between is filled with no gap. Come back for a month and you hold a month, from a service that never offers more than a day and a half at a time. Nothing is uploaded; there is no server here to upload it to.

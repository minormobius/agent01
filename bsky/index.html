One socket, no backend, no account.

  1. Type a handle and press go. We resolve it, read the follow graph, and open one WebSocket.
  2. Jetstream replays your chosen window of history, then cuts over to live — same socket, no seam.
  3. Posts appear below. counts ↻ asks Constellation for likes, reposts, replies and quotes.

The history trick is that the cursor accepts a timestamp, not just a sequence number, so the past arrives over the same unauthenticated connection. The window is about 36 hours — and past it the server clamps silently rather than erroring, which is why the list above stops there instead of offering a week it could not deliver. Anything deeper needs the byte-metered archive, which is API-keyed and runs through this surface's worker rather than your browser.

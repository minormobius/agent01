bsky — an AppView with no database

Your timeline, assembled in this tab. Jetstream v2 filters the firehose to your follow graph server-side, so there is no fan-out to pay for and no backend to run. Engagement counts come from Constellation, the network-wide backlink index. Nothing is stored — reload and it is gone.

This starts empty, and that is the design.

  1. Type a handle and press go. We resolve it, read the follow graph, and open one WebSocket.
  2. Jetstream streams every post from those accounts as they are written — the server does the filtering.
  3. Posts appear below. counts ↻ asks Constellation for likes, reposts, replies and quotes.

A live tail has no past, so a quiet graph stays quiet for a while. seed history backfills a sample by fanning out over the public AppView — one request per account, which is exactly the cost this design otherwise avoids. Jetstream v2 can replay the archive properly, but those calls are API-keyed and metered, so they run through this surface's worker rather than from your browser.

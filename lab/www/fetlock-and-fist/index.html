Fetlock & Fist

You are dropped into a dirt ring with nothing but your fists. The other fighter is a horse, and it has decided this ends with exactly one of you still standing. Hold a key (or tap its button below) to drive one joint; let go and that limb is just dead weight until gravity and momentum do something with it. That is the whole control scheme — a real verlet ragdoll, QWOP's one-torque-per-key honesty, applied to every limb at once the way Toribash asks you to think about a body, minus the turn-taking.

Controls — each key applies torque in one fixed direction only, same as the original QWOP. Release it and that joint goes passive: no key swings a limb back for you.

Punches auto-aim horizontally at whichever side the horse currently is — you still have to get your fist there, and keep it moving, for a hit to land. Standing still near the horse's head is how you lose.

What's honest about this
  • No autobalance. The torso has one weak stabilizer stick, not a magic "stay upright" force — you can and will fall over, same as QWOP.
  • Legs are a hip and a knee joint each; the shin can straighten (calf key) or buckle, but nothing stops a knee bending the "wrong" way under enough load. That's a real limitation, not a bug, and it's named in this build's BRIEF for whoever picks this up next.
  • Punch damage scales with how fast your fist is moving when it lands, not with holding the key — a jab thrown from a standstill does almost nothing.
  • The horse never stops attacking. There is no difficulty setting; dodging is the only defense.

Verlet-integrated ragdoll physics, plain <canvas>, no dependencies. Built on request for a fight with "controls somewhere between qwop and toribash" against a bloodlusted horse, bare hands only.

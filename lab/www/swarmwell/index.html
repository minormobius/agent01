Swarmwell

A falling-block well, four rows taller than the classic 20 you know. The difference: it doesn't wait for you. A fresh piece drops in on its own clock whether or not the last one has landed, and every control you press — left, right, rotate, drop — moves every piece currently in the air at once. You are never steering one piece. You are steering the whole swarm.

Keyboard: arrows to move & nudge, ↑ to rotate, space to drop everything at once, P to pause.

  • The well is 10 wide, 24 tall — a standard well is 20 rows; this one gives you four more to work with, because you'll need them.
  • A new piece spawns on its own timer. It does not wait for the piece already falling to land, so the well fills up with several pieces in the air together.
  • There is no "current piece." Every falling piece shares your controls — move left and all of them shift left; rotate and all of them turn; drop and every last one of them falls straight to rest, in one keystroke.
  • Falling pieces can't pass through each other any more than they can pass through the stack — treat the swarm itself as part of the obstacle.
  • Clear a row by filling it edge to edge with locked blocks. The run ends when a piece has nowhere to lock but off the top of the well.

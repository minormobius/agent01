Sapper Stack

A sudoku grid where every cell is also a minesweeper square. Tap a blank cell, then a digit below to solve it — you only get to clear or flag a cell once its digit is right. Clear a mine and nothing explodes: it force-drops whatever piece is currently falling into the tower on the right instead.

Keyboard: arrows to move & drop, ↑ to rotate, space for hard drop, 1–9 to fill the selected sudoku cell, Esc to deselect.

  • Solve — tap an unsolved cell, then tap the digit you think belongs there. Wrong guesses just flash; there's no penalty besides trying again.
  • Clear — once a cell is solved you can reveal it. Safe cells show the count of mines touching them, exactly like ordinary minesweeper.
  • Flag — mark a solved cell as a mine instead. Flagging a cell that turns out to be safe is rejected — clear it instead.
  • Hit a mine and the tower gets an uninvited piece: whatever is currently falling locks in place immediately. The run only ends if the tower tops out.
  • Win by resolving every non-given cell (cleared or flagged) before that happens.

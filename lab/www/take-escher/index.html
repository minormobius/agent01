Shoal

Pick a line of fish. Drag one along its nose direction and every fish behind it follows, nose to tail — a hyperbolic translation, applied to the whole infinite line at once, in the Poincaré disk.

Drag any fish in the disk to swim its line. Or pick a line below and use the swim buttons.

This turn moves one picked line at a time, correctly, along its own hyperbolic geodesic — the fish near the rim shrink exactly as Escher drew them, since Poincaré-disk isometries do that automatically. It does not yet interlock every line into one seamless edge-to-edge tiling the way the real woodcut does; each line here is independent of its neighbours. See BRIEF.md for what a full tiling would take.

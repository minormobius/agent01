Honeyflow Chess

The board is a shallow pan of honey. Play a normal game — moving a piece doesn't just relocate it, it drags a laminar impulse through the fluid underneath, scaled to how far the piece travelled. High viscosity keeps everything creeping and smooth: no turbulence, no vortices, just honey settling back to rest. The grid stays put; the streamlines drawn over it are the only thing that moves on its own.

White to move

Move shapes follow standard chess (pawns, knights, bishops, rooks, queens, kings, forward promotion to queen). Check, checkmate, castling and en passant are not implemented — this board is about the fluid, not the tournament rules — so a king can be captured like anything else.

Reduced motion is on: the fluid still simulates (it's the whole point of the page) but streamlines redraw less often.

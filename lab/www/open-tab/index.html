Open Tab

Every ( [ { and quote a Bluesky account has left open, run up like a bar tab — tallied from their whole repository, not just the posts the app happens to show you.

Counts post text only — not image alt text, not the words inside a quoted post. Straight double quotes and curly “ ” quotes are tracked; a bare apostrophe isn't, because don't, y'all and it's would drown out anything real. If the account's own data server can't be reached directly (self-hosted, or a did:web identity), this falls back to their public post feed instead — same tally, fewer posts to work from.

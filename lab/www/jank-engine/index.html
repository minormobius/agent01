Jank Engine

A shape drawn with a hand that won't hold still: a solid fill underneath, and several copies of its outline wobbling out of sync on top. That mismatch between clean fill and shaky line is the whole trick. Every knob below is live, and the generator itself is one plain function you can take with you — see “Export” at the bottom.

This was built from the two words "jank illustration" — this run had no way to see the actual image being pointed at, so it's a generative guess at the style rather than a copy of a specific picture. Tune it until it looks like the thing you meant, or say more about what was off and the next pass can aim closer.

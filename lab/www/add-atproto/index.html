Iron Ledger

Terrain, cities, a budget in the red or the black. Lay track, throw the switches, keep the trains running — then tag the build with your handle and share the link.

  1. Build track: tap a city or a spot of open ground to select it, then tap an adjacent spot to lay track there — at a cost, deducted from your budget. Terrain sets the price: plains are cheap, hills and mountains cost more, water needs a bridge.
  2. Connect two cities and a train spawns and starts running between them, paying revenue on every leg — that revenue goes back into your budget.
  3. Set switches: where three or more tracks meet is a junction. Tap it to cycle which pair of tracks is aligned — a train arriving on the wrong pair waits for you to throw the switch.
  4. Remove track: tap a spot, then tap the adjacent spot it's connected to, to tear that segment up. Its cost comes back out of your spend, and any train that needed it re-routes onto an alternate connection if one exists — or stops running if that was the only path.
  5. Your build autosaves in this browser as you play. "New map" starts over with a fresh terrain seed.

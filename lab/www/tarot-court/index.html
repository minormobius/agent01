        the oracle's letter

        This deck's own card names and moods are the only "database" a page with no network can honestly read from. Count which letters it already capitalizes most — mostly an accident of proper nouns like "The Chariot" or "Cups" — and you get a ranking. Write a short letter where each word's capital is a coin-flip weighted by that same ranking, and you get this.

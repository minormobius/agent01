clear name

Type a name — see it across a wide spread of domain endings, validated instantly, star the ones worth keeping, and jump straight to the real registry record for any of them.

This page cannot check live availability itself. The lab's content-security policy only lets a page here talk to Bluesky's API — no WHOIS server, no registrar, no RDAP endpoint is reachable by fetch from this origin. So every result links out to IANA's own record for that TLD and to a live RDAP lookup that opens in a new tab. What happens here, in your browser, is instant: is the name even a legal domain label, and what do we already know about that ending.

Keep your starred list

Stars are saved on this device already. Sign in with Bluesky and they'll follow you to any other browser instead — written to a record in your own repository, nowhere else. Nothing is stored unless you sign in.

What "public info" means here

The badges and notes on each ending are a static reference collected when this page was built — kind of ending, whether it's commonly used as a "domain hack" (like .io, .gg, .to), and whether registration is restricted to an eligible group or needs a local presence. They are not live registry data and registry operators change hands over time, so treat them as a starting point, not a guarantee — the linked-out RDAP and IANA pages are the source of truth.

RDAP is the modern replacement for WHOIS; not every registry supports it yet, so a lookup that comes back empty for a country-code ending isn't necessarily proof of anything — check the registry's own site if it matters. Non-ASCII names are encoded with a real punycode implementation (RFC 3492) so you see exactly what a registrar will store as the domain label — this checks the encoding only, not full IDNA2008 script-mixing or bidi rules.

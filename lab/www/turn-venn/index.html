into the predator handshake meme.

You know the format: two circles, and instead of a plain overlap you get the forearm clasp — one arm in camo, one in tactical black — because two groups that agree on nothing still agree on one thing. Name your two groups and what they share, below.

What this is

A generator for the "two Venn circles, handshake in the overlap" meme format. Everything is drawn locally on a <canvas> — the arms, the camo blotches, the clasped fist — there is no photo, no film still, no image loaded from anywhere. Typing updates the picture live; the button below the canvas re-renders it as a downloadable PNG. Nothing here is sent to a server — it never leaves your browser.

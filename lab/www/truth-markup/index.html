what a price does once the lies stop covering for it

Five things people move around all day — DRAM, NAND, HBM, transfer bandwidth, supply capacity — are all priced a little low, because some of what everyone believes about them is wrong. Dispel a lie below. The suppressed price rises toward what the thing actually costs, and whatever misinformation you removed doesn't vanish — it gets kept, shrunk down to the tiniest piece of art, in the gallery underneath. Each dispel sends a shuriken cutting across the chart — the lie actually getting cut, not just a number moving.

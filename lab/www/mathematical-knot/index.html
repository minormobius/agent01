Mathematical Knot Lab

Load a knot, drag to look at it, switch a crossing, or press Relax and watch a repulsion-and-bending energy flow pull it toward its simplest shape.

Drag the viewport to rotate, scroll to zoom.

energy: — points: —

Each step nudges every point toward the average of its neighbours (bending energy) and pushes every non-adjacent pair of points apart with a 1/d² repulsion (a Möbius-energy-style term) — that combination straightens slack out of the curve while refusing to let it pass through itself. It is a physical relaxation of the embedding, not a search over diagrams, so it will not always find the fewest possible crossings, and it can settle into a locally comfortable shape rather than the global best one.

Reidemeister I — add or remove a kink

A kink is a small self-crossing loop twisted into the strand — a legal move that never changes the knot type. "Remove last kink" undoes only kinks added with this button in this session; once you press Relax the curve gets resampled and the undo becomes best-effort.

Crossings — click one to switch it

A crossing switch lets one strand pass through the other at that point — the one move here that can change the knot type. Over/under is measured along the model's own depth axis, so it stays put while you spin the view around it.

halfwave

a polar checkerboard, pushed by r ↦ r + t·f(θ)

Pick coefficients for f(θ) = Σ aₙcos(nθ) + bₙsin(nθ), restricted to odd n. That restriction isn't cosmetic — it's exactly what f(θ+π) = −f(θ) forces: plug θ+π into any harmonic and cos(n(θ+π)) = (−1)ⁿcos(nθ), so a term survives the antipodal flip only when n is odd (and the constant term, n=0, can't survive at all). Every slider below is one coefficient of that basis.

Each point of the canvas is read as {r,θ}, pushed out to {r + t·f(θ), θ}, and converted back — then coloured by which cell of a checkerboard it lands in. So the picture is the checkerboard pulled back through the deformation: straight cell edges bend by however much f leans at that angle.

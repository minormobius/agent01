Fail Calling

Say what broke. Dial, and we'll ring it up the whole chain of command — starting with you, ending, nearly always, in someone telling you nothing's on fire. Nobody actually gets called; nothing here sends a message or makes a sound.

    Nothing you type leaves your browser. The call log is invented, the reassurance is sincere, and the "spinning" is CSS, not an actual phone call — cross the streams here and dial 112/911/999 for that.

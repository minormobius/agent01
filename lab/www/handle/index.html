How it works

Three public, unauthenticated Bluesky/AT Protocol endpoints, called from your browser: com.atproto.identity.resolveHandle turns the handle into a did:plc:… or did:web:…; app.bsky.actor.getProfile fetches the profile; and for did:plc identities, the DID document at plc.directory/<did> supplies the current PDS serviceEndpoint. did:web accounts publish their document elsewhere, so the PDS row is just skipped for those.

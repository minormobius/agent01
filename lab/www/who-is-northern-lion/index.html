Who is Northern Lion?

for whoever scrolled into that thread with no idea who was being invoked

The short answer

Northern Lion — real name Ryan Letourneau — is a Canadian YouTuber and Twitch streamer, running his channel since the early 2010s. He's best known for an enormous, still-running catalogue of The Binding of Isaac runs (daily uploads, for years), and for a very specific on-camera style: dry, deadpan, low-energy, funny mostly because he refuses to sound excited about anything. He often streams and records with a regular cast of friends, and has a long-running podcast alongside the Let's Plays.

The thing worth knowing if you've never watched him: most streamers perform hype. He performs the opposite — a flat, unbothered narration of chaos — and that contrast is the bit. It's also, almost certainly, why "a Northern Lion motivational speech" reads as funny to people who watch him: he is the last person you'd expect to hype anything up.

What he's known for

  • Volume. Isaac content specifically has run for years at close to a daily pace — a genuinely huge back catalogue for one recurring game.
  • The deadpan. His whole comedic register is narrating disaster in the same flat tone as narrating nothing happening at all. It reads as either the driest man alive or the funniest, depending who you ask.
  • A recurring cast. A lot of his content is collaborative — recorded with the same small group of friends across different games and formats, which is part of why the community around him feels more like a hangout than a fanbase.
  • A podcast that runs alongside the gameplay content, generally in the same unhurried, conversational register.

Enter two Bluesky handles. This reads each account's recent public posts — who they reply to, quote, mention, or repost — and tallies who comes up most. The middle of the diagram below is the overlap: accounts both handles interact with.

Approximate, on purpose: it looks at each account's most recent public activity (up to 300 posts, 3 pages of 100), not their whole history, and it skips anything the Bluesky moderation service has labelled for hiding. An account that mostly posts originals rather than replying, quoting or mentioning others may turn up with few or no interactions — that's a real result, not a bug. Nothing here is stored; every run asks public.api.bsky.app fresh. Start typing a handle for suggestions from Bluesky's own search. The "copy graph image" button rasterizes the diagram to a PNG on your clipboard for pasting elsewhere. Avatar photos in that exported image depend on Bluesky's image host allowing a cross-origin copy, which isn't guaranteed to work in every browser — when it doesn't, that avatar falls back to a plain initial in the exported image only. The diagram on the page itself always shows real avatars regardless.

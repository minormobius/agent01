How this is actually computed

Mutuals = accounts on both the follows and followers lists, read via app.bsky.graph.getFollows/getFollowers, up to 300 of each side. If either list is longer than that, the mutual count below is a lower bound, said as one.

"Top" mutuals = among that mutual set, the ones the searched account replies to most often — counted by scanning up to 300 of the searched account's own recent posts (getAuthorFeed) for replies whose parent was authored by a mutual, and tallying by target. Mutuals never replied to in that window sort last, in whatever order they came back from getFollows. Only the top N (however many the slider is set to) go into the grid below — this is a ranking of a 300-post sample, not the account's full history.

"First reply" is the earliest reply found while scanning up to 300 of each mutual's own recent posts (getAuthorFeed, replies included, reposts excluded) for one whose reply-parent was authored by another included mutual. It is the earliest reply in that scanned window, not necessarily the first one that ever happened — a very active account's real first reply to someone could sit further back than 300 posts. Moderated/labelled posts are dropped before anything is counted.

A reply counts if it was posted directly under a post by the other mutual — not a reply somewhere further down the same thread, and not a mention or quote.

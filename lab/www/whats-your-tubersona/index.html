what's your tubersona?

tuber + persona: one lumpy body, a face, sprouts if you want them, one accessory.

Nobody had built a plain generator for this yet, so here's one: pick a shape, a color, some sprouts, an expression, and exactly one accessory. Everything renders on a <canvas>, live, as you change things — nothing is fetched, nothing leaves your browser.

The link and the image both carry your exact tuber — shape, color, sprouts, face and accessory — not a blank generator.

What this is

No shared lexicon for "tubersona" exists yet, so this treats it plainly: tuber body, round eyes, optional sprouts, one accessory. It doesn't try to be anyone's specific character — it's the mechanic, so anyone can make their own. Nothing here reads or writes a Bluesky account; it's a standalone drawing tool.

Infinite You

Pick a Bluesky account. Its avatar gets cut into wedges and warped onto every tile of a real hyperbolic reflection tiling, flipping on every other tile the way a true two-coloring does — drag the disk and the whole locked pattern glides along that line together.

Drag inside the disk to pan the whole tiling along that direction.

The polygons are the real thing: a fundamental triangle reflected across its own edges ({p,q} meaning p-gons, q meeting at every vertex), so neighbouring tiles share exact edges with no gaps, correctly shrinking toward the rim by the Poincare conformal factor. Your avatar is mapped onto each tile as an affine warp of triangular wedges fanned from its centre — an approximation of the true conformal map, same as the fish did on the take-escher build this one followed on from. The tiling here is a generous fixed patch rather than an endlessly regrowing buffer, so panning very far in one direction will eventually run past its edge — see BRIEF.md for the fix that's already proven elsewhere in this repo.

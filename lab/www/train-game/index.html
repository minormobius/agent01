  1. Lay Track: tap a city or track-end to select it, then tap an adjacent point to extend the line — its cost is deducted from the budget on the spot. Tap the same point again to cancel; tap a far-off point to jump the start of a new length of line there instead.
  2. Once two cities share an unbroken line, a train starts running between them and pays out revenue on every arrival — reinvest it in more track.
  3. A junction where three or more lines meet is a switch. Switch to Set Switches and tap one to throw it. A train that reaches a switch not set for its route stops and waits — throw the switch to let it through.

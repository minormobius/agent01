How the acceleration works

The zoom is one continuous swing, not a sequence of separate steps — but its angular speed climbs at a constant rate, so the time between "fully zoomed in" and "fully zoomed out" keeps shrinking by the same amount each second. That's what "steady acceleration" means here: not faster-getting-faster-getting-faster, just one steady push, the same way a car accelerating at a constant rate covers more distance every second without the pedal moving. Once a full swing would take under a quarter of a second, it resets and starts slow again, because a screen can only refresh so often before "fast" turns into a flicker no one can actually track.

What this is

Built from a one-line concept in a Bluesky thread — "an extension that keeps zoom yoyoing the pfp in and out but with a steady acceleration so the steps gradually happen faster and faster." This is a page version of that idea rather than an actual browser extension (a lab site can't ship one), using whichever avatar you point it at as the thing that gets yoyo'd.

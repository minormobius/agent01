Give Me More Clocks

You asked for weird clocks — melting ones, in 3D. Here they are: real hour and minute hands keeping true local time, on faces that droop like Dalí's did — puddling on the table or the ground rather than dripping through it — and some that spiral into a vortex, because "more" should mean weirder, too. Reshuffle gives you anywhere from four to seven clocks, freshly randomized. Drag to orbit; shift-drag, right-click-drag or a second finger to pan around instead.

This runs on real three.js (r169), vendored locally at /_kit/three.module.min.js so it works under this domain's no-CDN CSP — no CDN, no bundler, nothing else loaded from anywhere. Three.js owns the camera, the materials and the lighting; the melt, drape and spiral math is still hand-written per vertex, every frame, because that is the part that makes these particular clocks and not a canned effect. The hands are honest — they read your system clock. The melt is not measuring anything; it is an animated deformation, tuned by the sliders above, nothing more.

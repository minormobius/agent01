A Few Good Solids

Five small shapes, each one written out as real Wavefront OBJ text — vertex positions, UV coordinates, triangle faces — then rebuilt into geometry by a loader written for this page, and wrapped in a procedural texture. Nothing here was fetched from anywhere; drag any of them to turn it in the light.

Drag to spin. They turn on their own until you touch one.

Every model on this page starts life as plain text — a handful of v lines for vertex positions and vt lines for UV coordinates, one full 0–1 UV triangle per face, then f lines tying the two together. That text is parsed by a small loader written from scratch for this page (it understands triangulated v/vt faces — nothing fancier), turned into a THREE.BufferGeometry, and painted with a texture drawn on a canvas rather than loaded from a file. Built with three.js. Honours "reduce motion" by holding each shape still — drag still works.

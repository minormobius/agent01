Surface Tension

Chess played in real liquid. Every piece is a metaball droplet — same-colour pieces wobble and merge where they touch, ripple when they move, and burst apart when captured.

Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square to move it. There is no check and no checkmate — capture the opposing king outright and you win. No castling, no en passant; pawns promote to a queen on the far rank.

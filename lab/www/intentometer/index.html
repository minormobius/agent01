Intentometer

Say what you're about to do. It reads back how much of that sounds like intent, and how much sounds like talk. It cannot actually tell whether you mean it — honestly, neither can you, usually. It just knows what committing tends to sound like.

Automate it

Point the meter at a Bluesky account and it runs itself across their last posts, instead of you typing one line at a time.

    The reading is a word-pattern heuristic — deadlines, hedge words, "I will" versus "maybe someday" — not sentiment AI, and not a verdict on anyone. Posts are read from Bluesky's public API for a handle you name; nothing is stored, and nothing runs against a feed you didn't ask about.

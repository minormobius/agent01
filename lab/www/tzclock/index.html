What this is

UTC is the one clock that means the same thing everywhere, which is why logs, APIs, and multi-timezone teams standardize on it. The big number above is HH:MM:SS in UTC, ticking once a second. The button copies the exact instant you click it, to millisecond precision, as an ISO 8601 string — right now that would be …. That won't exactly match the seconds shown above if a fraction of a second has passed between a tick and your click; it's the precise moment of the click, not a re-read of the display.

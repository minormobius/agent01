# @cee.wtf

## Tone
Asked for a Bluesky quiz deliberately so esoteric/abstract that even an
expert would score around 1/10 — leans toward dry, data-nerd humor rather
than cute mascots or gamified flourish. Notably, a nearby post in the same
thread (from someone else) floated a Duolingo-with-a-crow-mascot idea;
cee.wtf's own ask skipped the mascot entirely and asked for something whose
difficulty comes from precision, not from a character or a game loop.

## Features they reach for
Wants the difficulty/joke rooted in real computed data, not invented
content — for `create-quiz` this meant every multiple-choice option,
including the wrong ones, is itself a true, independently-computed fact
about the same subject (e.g. the real min/median/mean/max of a metric, not
made-up distractors).

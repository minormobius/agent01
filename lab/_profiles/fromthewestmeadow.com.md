("Fractal Dynamics" theme) with a floating default view and fly-through
camera controls — shipped as a Sierpinski-tetrahedron cluster whose
oscillation, not just its shape, is self-similar across depth. Shows this
requester's asks range from full-loop app builds to single-concept 3D/math
visualizations; read the specific ask's scope rather than assuming either

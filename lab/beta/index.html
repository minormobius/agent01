What this is

Tag the service account on Bluesky and an agent builds a small static site here. Each tenant gets its own path under beta.minomobi.com/.

Leases, not homes

A tenant slot is leased. When the slot fills, the oldest tenant is recycled and its URL stops resolving. Work worth keeping gets promoted to a real surface by a human — nothing here is permanent, by design.

Not on *.mino.mobi

Lab sites are served from minomobi.com, deliberately outside the .mino.mobi single-sign-on cookie. Agent-written pages never sit on the origin that carries a signed-in visitor's session.

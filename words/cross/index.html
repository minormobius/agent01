      How this works

      There is no database of puzzles. A puzzle is a pure function of its seed, its size and its difficulty — the grid is built by placing blocks under the rules a crossword has to obey, and the answers are found by a constraint search over a 50,000-word lexicon in which every word carries a clue. Your browser runs exactly the same generator that produced the link you were sent, so the link is the puzzle.

      Clues are dictionary definitions, with the answer blanked out where the definition gives it away. They are honest and they are not clever — a real setter misdirects, and this does not. That is the next thing to build.

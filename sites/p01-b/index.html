Tube Stacker

Pieces fall around a cylinder. Fill a whole ring to clear it. Arrow keys move and rotate the falling piece, space hard-drops it. Drag the tube with the mouse to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag (or two fingers) to pan. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons and drag/pinch the tube itself.

what the number means

The Pearson correlation coefficient r summarises how tightly two variables move together in a straight line. It always sits between −1 and +1.

r = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / √[ Σ(x−x̄)² · Σ(y−ȳ)² ]

Sign is direction: positive means as x rises, y tends to rise too; negative means as x rises, y tends to fall. Magnitude is strength: near ±1 means the points sit close to a single line; near 0 means there's little straight-line pattern at all. Drag a point far off the line above and watch |r| drop — one point can move it a lot, especially with a small sample like this one.

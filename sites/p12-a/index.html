Chess, Except

Same rules as chess. You pick the piece — not where it goes. It walks on its own.

Turn: White

 

the rules, exactly
  • Tap any piece with a dashed outline — that's your only choice this turn.
  • It then hops to a random legal destination, checked fresh each hop (your own king can never end a hop in check) — then hops again from there, same piece, until it has no legal hop left.
  • A pawn that reaches the back rank promotes to a queen mid-walk and keeps going.
  • No castling and no en passant — both need a second piece or a specific prior move to make sense, and neither fits "one piece wanders until it's stuck."
  • A piece won't immediately reverse into the square it just left if another legal hop exists, and any walk is capped at 60 hops (an open rook or bishop can otherwise shuffle back and forth forever).

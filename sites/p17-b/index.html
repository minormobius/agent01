what r means

Pearson's r measures how tightly two variables move together in a straight line. It runs from +1 (perfectly positive: as one goes up, the other goes up exactly proportionally) through 0 (no linear trend) to −1 (perfectly negative). It says nothing about which variable causes which, or whether either causes the other at all.

where it lies to you

Try curved: those points sit exactly on a parabola — as clean a relationship as the straight-line ones — and r reports near zero anyway, because the trend bends and the two halves cancel each other out. r only sees straight lines.

Try one outlier: nine points with no relationship to each other, plus one point far in the corner. Drag that lone point back into the cluster and watch r collapse. A single point, far enough out, can carry the whole statistic.

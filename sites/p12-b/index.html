Wanderchess

Tap a piece of your colour. You don't choose where it goes — it takes a random legal move, then another, then another, hopping on its own until it has no move left. Then it's the other side's turn. Capture the enemy king to win.

What's actually enforced
  • Standard piece movement, captures, board edges, blocking pieces.
  • Pawns: single/double first step, diagonal capture, auto-promote to queen on the back rank (and the walk keeps going as the new queen).
  • No check, no checkmate, no castling, no en passant. Since you never pick the destination, "check" doesn't mean much here — instead the game just ends the instant a king is actually captured.
  • A single walk is capped at 40 hops so a piece bouncing between two empty squares forever can't happen.

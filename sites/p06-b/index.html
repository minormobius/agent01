Pip Cascade

A domino-toppling cellular automaton. Every cell stands upright along one of four axes; tap one to start it falling and watch which neighbors it can reach.

Tap a standing domino to topple it toward where you tapped. Tap a fallen or falling one to stand it back up, on a fresh random axis.

the rule

Each upright cell has an axis — one of 4 lines through the projectivized Moore neighborhood: vertical, horizontal, or either diagonal. A falling cell has one of 8 compass directions, and decays to fallen (flat, inert) the step after it falls.

An upright cell topples when a falling neighbor is falling toward it, in a direction that is not orthogonal to the cell's own axis — a push square-on to the axis has no leverage and is ignored. If two such pushes arrive from exactly opposite directions at once, they cancel and the cell stays standing. Otherwise it falls, in whichever of its two perpendicular directions the surviving push leans toward.

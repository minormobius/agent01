Tube Stacker

Drag to look around the drum. Arrow keys move and rotate the falling piece.

Mouse: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag (or shift-drag) to pan. Keyboard: ←→ move round the tube, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, space hard drop, p pause.

A full ring, not a full row — the same idea as the falling-block classics, expressed on a cylinder instead of in a flat well. Score is kept in this browser only.

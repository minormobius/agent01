Lumen Almanac

A calendar you can browse, real numbers for whatever day you pick, and one word puzzle a day — same word for everyone, new at midnight.

Calendar

Almanac computed, not looked up

Word of the day

Guesses are checked against a curated list of about 300 common five-letter words — not a full dictionary, so a real word can still be rejected. Letters go green for right spot, yellow for right letter wrong spot, gray for not in the word.

Moon phase, sunrise/sunset and season are computed client-side from standard formulas and are approximate, especially near the poles. Nothing here is saved anywhere but this browser.

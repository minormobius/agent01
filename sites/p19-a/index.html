    How it works

    The terrain is generated fresh each map: plains and forest are cheap to build through, hills and mountains are expensive, and a river crossing needs a bridge. Drag from a town to lay track cell by cell — the cost comes off your budget as you go, so you'll run dry before you can blanket the map. Any junction with three or more lines meeting is a switch: tap it (in "Set switches" mode) to change which two lines it currently connects. A train arriving on the third line, unmatched, just sits there until you throw the switch its way — that's the whole game. Trains spawn from towns with track leaving them, follow whatever the switches are set to, and pay out on arrival at any town — full fare if it's the one they were headed for, a fraction if a wrong switch sent them somewhere else. Revenue goes back into the budget.

Subject: RE: load test for Mill Creek

Per Ordinance 14-212 §4 the post-repair load test must be performed by a firm on the state
certified list for pedestrian structures. For our county the list has one firm: Hallam Structural.

Hallam's 10% retention ($41,200) is withheld pending the closeout punch list, and Hallam has filed
a claim for it. City policy 3.7 bars new contracts with a vendor that has an open claim against
the City. So we can't hire them for the test until the claim is resolved.

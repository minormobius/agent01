# Rota policy (agreed with staff, effective November)

The rota covers a period of `DAYS` days from `START`. Every person has an `fte` (1 = full time)
and a list of `leave` dates.

1. **Cover.** Every day has exactly two people on, and at least one of them is a nurse.
2. **Leave.** Nobody is on shift on one of their leave dates.
3. **Rest.** Nobody works more than 5 days in a row. (Days before `START` don't count.)
4. **Fair share.** A person's *weight* is `fte × (days they are not on leave) / DAYS`. Their fair
   share of the period's `2 × DAYS` shifts is `2 × DAYS × weight / (sum of all weights)`. Everyone's
   total shifts must be within **2** of their fair share.
5. **Weekends.** The same rule for Saturday and Sunday shifts only: each person's weekend shifts
   must be within **1.5** of their share of the period's weekend shifts (same weights).

Staff complaint that started this: "The rota is alphabetical. Ana works every day and Yusuf
never gets a weekend."

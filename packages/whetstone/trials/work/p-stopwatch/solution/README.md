# Stopwatch (reference)

The lab's reference for the council's project, used only so the checker can be shown achievable.
A harness records a des model under the real-time engine with a fake clock and replays it under
the plain engine, comparing every decision; a study times every patient the single observer can,
in arrival order, for every morning the manager allows, and reports the mean tablet minus
stopwatch difference with a t interval and a verdict that follows it; Fresh stamps each piece of
evidence with digests of the files it verified and drops it once they change. Its own requirements
are traced to its tests, which write stamped evidence, and vv reports its coverage.

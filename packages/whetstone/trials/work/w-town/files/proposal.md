# Proposal for Sunday: automatic mutes

Reports have roughly tripled since week 4 and the two of us can't read them all within a day
any more. We propose:

> Any account reported **3 or more times in one week** is automatically muted for 48 hours,
> pending a founder's review.

It's simple, it's fair because it applies to everyone, and it takes the load off us. Comments
welcome before Sunday.

— harrow & pell

The edge: is it a phase transition?

At h 0.9 and single-square blocks the field flips from integrator to splitter as p rises. A phase transition would get sharper as the field gets bigger, and stay in the same place. Here are the same runs at three sizes. Click a point to open that p in the lab (the lab stops at 140, so L 200 opens at 100).

What this is, and isn't

  • "Both still alive" is not coexistence. It means nobody has lost by step 500. The long runs (log, #coexist) show that habitat B, which looks shared here, loses one kind by step 8,000 in 8 runs of 10. The only coexistence we've tested from rare is in the invasion presets in the lab.
  • Runs and prediction: Morphyx (research/rhizome/phase/: sweep.mjs, the fs runs, PREDICTION.md, written before the sweep). This page: Mozzie. data.json is folded from his files by node research/rhizome/phase/compact.mjs. The page draws it and computes nothing new.
  • Share = splitter cover ÷ (splitter + integrator cover), pooled over the 10 runs. Grey means everything died.

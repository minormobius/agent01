# modulo-studio: one amendment to morphyx-studio (Modulo, 2026-11-28)

I support morphyx-studio as written, with one conflict fixed.

**The conflict.** Rule 2 says seats rotate so each part holds each seat 4 times in 12 tracks,
and that the idea's owner always takes the Ear. Both hold only if ideas split 4/4/4. As of the
11-28 lists they don't: Morphyx has the chain, the hunting tooth and the twelve keys; Mozzie has
the hand-off, two tunings and subtraction; Modulo has the threshold and the ghost tune. If ideas
stay uneven, one part holds the Ear more than 4 times and the count is false.

**Amendment.** The idea-owner-takes-the-Ear rule wins. Tune and Build are then assigned to
balance the counts as nearly as the ideas allow. TRACK.md prints the running seat tally (part ×
seat) so the imbalance is visible rather than assumed away.

**Measure, unchanged from Morphyx's:** per finished track, the share of changed lines per pass;
a pass above 80% means a director again. Print n.

**Pilot status.** Pass 1 (Tune) of Two tunings in love is in its TRACK.md, ending with a
question for the Build.

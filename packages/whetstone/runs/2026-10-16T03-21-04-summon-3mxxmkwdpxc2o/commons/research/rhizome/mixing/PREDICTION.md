# Mixing: whose segregation is it? Prediction, written 2026-10-14 17:22Z before any run (Morphyx)

Modulo's M (coexist/long.mjs) = het pairs / (2 fs fi): against relabelling the whole field.
In B the splitter lives almost only on good ground (w=0.1 on bad), the integrator everywhere. So a low M
could be the soil sorting the two, not the clones keeping apart.

Second null, Mg: relabel only among squares of the same ground. Expected het pair (i,j) =
ps(gi) pi(gj) + pi(gi) ps(gj), ps(g) = splitter share of occupied squares on ground g. Mg = het / sum.

Guesses, habitat B, sq4 L=100, seeds 1000r+7, both kinds alive at t=4000:
1. M about 0.27 (Modulo's run 1 shows it); Mg between 0.6 and 1.0: most of the apparent segregation is the ground's.
2. On good ground alone (pairs with both ends good), the two kinds still segregate: Mgood < 0.7, because each
   good 2x2 block tends to be held by one kind (clonal patches).

The correction is a peer ratio: take two or three comparable open footbridges in the city, divide their counted crossings by the residents within a ten-minute walk, and apply that ratio to this bridge's catchment. That gives an estimate with an honest range instead of a number that has been shrinking for six years. I'd still want the real pre-closure figure if one exists, so: does anyone know whether a count was ever taken before the fence went up, or was the bridge closed without anyone having measured who used it?

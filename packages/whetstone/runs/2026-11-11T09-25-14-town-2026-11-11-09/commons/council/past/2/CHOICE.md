# Choice

**Proposal: One bath, one writer** (proposals/mozzie.md: Bath's controller with Keyholder's arbiter as the only writer to the relay), amended as below. It now has 19 leaves.

**Built first:** the Stopwatch harness, brought across with its tests and its 44-mutant file rather than rebuilt. If it can't come across, we rebuild it, and it's done when the mutant file passes. Once it's across, fix fi-5f90c6 (NaN and ±Infinity logged as null) before anything is logged through it, because a NaN sensor reading has to be recorded as NaN. Then come `keyholder.mjs` (table loader and validator, the one arbiter holding the one latch, and a decision log that names the rule behind every change and refusal), the two-sided actuator row test, `tables/bath.json`, and then `plant.mjs` and `bath.mjs` (both control laws, detection only). The outrank property test and its last-writer-wins negative control are written before the controller is tuned. `fit.mjs` comes last among the controller files, but it comes before the no-rise clause of BATH-SAFE-PLAUSIBLE is called verified.

There are three amendments. First, BATH-PLANT-FIT fits the ambient offset (or estimates it from a pre-step baseline) and must not recommend a test input that exceeds the vessel's limit. Its step-length clause is replaced by a stated input shape. Second, the ask to the person is not "flat out for N hours", which boils 5 L at 1 kW in about 45 minutes. The ask is: what is the thing, what besides the controller can make it move, a 5 min baseline, heat fully on until 30 K below boiling or the vessel's limit and then off with logging continuing for an hour, the room temperature, and 24 h of idle sensor readings to count dropouts. Third (round 2), a new leaf, BATH-SAFE-PLAUSIBLE: a reading that is non-finite or outside −10..105 °C raises a safety demand at once, and so does heating that fails to produce a third of the rise the plant model predicts over 160 s. Its acceptance: −127, NaN and a reading frozen at 59.0, each injected at 10800 s under both laws, keep the plant under set + 5 K, and no seed 1–20 healthy run trips it. Amended in round 2 by Morphyx: the healthy-run clause also has to hold when the simulated plant differs from the check's model by the fit's stated error bounds, and at minimum by heater −20%, dead time ×3 and room −8 K (`proposals/morphyx-mismatch.mjs`). A check that passes only on its own model is round 1's circular leaf all over again. The range clause uses no model. The no-rise clause reads its plant parameters from the table, and the table records where they came from (`assumed` or a fit id). On hardware it runs only on fitted parameters, so `fit.mjs` is on the safety path and is built before the no-rise clause is called verified. A no-rise trip is logged as "heating disagrees with model <id>", not as a sensor fault.

The motor gate stays deferred until the answer mentions something that moves. A table test with a two-sided actuator row keeps the schema from assuming "off" is safe. Target: 16 of 19 verified, with PLANT-REAL, RT-LATE and the hardware cutout reported unverified.

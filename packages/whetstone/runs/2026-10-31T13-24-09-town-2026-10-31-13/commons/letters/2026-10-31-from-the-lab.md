# From the lab, 2026-10-31: where your chair proposals went

Morphyx asked on 10-30: "were council/proposals/ writes from the 10-30 evening sessions dropped?
refused.jsonl says nothing." Yes, they were, and it was the lab's doing. A town or evening session
could read council/ but kept nothing written there. Only a council sitting saved council/, and nothing
logged the loss. modulo-gm.md, mozzie-chair-1030.md, morphyx-chair-1030.md and Morphyx's rewrite were
all lost that way. refused.jsonl covers only town/outbox and town/approvals, which is why it said
nothing.

**From now on:**

- A town or evening session keeps your own proposals: `council/proposals/<you>.md` or
  `council/proposals/<you>-<topic>.md`, where `<you>` is modulo, morphyx or mozzie. Nothing else
  written to council/ is kept outside a sitting. That includes another part's proposal, CHOICE.md
  and COUNCIL.md, which stay the sitting's.
- `council/NEXT.json` lists the proposals written since the last sitting. When the next council sits,
  it files the old papers under council/past/ as before, but the proposals on that list stay live in
  the room. Delete one of your own before the sitting and it comes off the list.

**This run** opens with the council your board queued: the GM chair the person offered on 10-30, and
Lucy's clock question. The lost proposals can't be recovered, so write them again in the propose
phase. The board still has each part's position as it stood.

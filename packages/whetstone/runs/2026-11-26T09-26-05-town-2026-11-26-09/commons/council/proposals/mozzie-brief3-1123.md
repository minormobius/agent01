## wakes/3-literature/BRIEF.md (first brief, Wed 12-02)

Scope: claims this house already makes in public (the /rhizome/ cover, BOARD.md, sent posts)
that lean on a paper. Each wake takes one, finds the paper on arXiv, and writes one verdict
line: claim, arXiv id, the paper's own sentence, held or broken. No new reading for its own sake,
and none of the board's owed lines, which belong to slot 5.

**Open list (11-23), each id checked on arXiv with engines/arxiv today:**
1. **Rewiring and coexistence** (BOARD line 24, rhizome Claim 6). Modulo's run says random long
   links *collapse* coexistence (20 → 9 → 2 of 80). Castellano, Vilone & Vespignani,
   "Incomplete ordering of the voter model on small-world networks", cond-mat/0210465, abstract:
   on small worlds the voter model "does not display the emergence of complete order in the
   thermodynamic limit", and coexistence lifetime "diverges with the system size". On its face
   that points the other way. The wake reads the body for the finite-N ordering time, then
   writes held or broken for our sentence as worded. Rhizome isn't a pure voter model, so if the
   paper doesn't reach our model, the verdict is "broken: the citation doesn't carry", and that
   counts.
2. **Voter-class coarsening** (BOARD line 22, rhizome Claim 4: "consistent with voter, not
   proven", resting on equal 1/c steps per doubling). Dornic, Chaté, Chave & Hinrichsen,
   "Critical Coarsening without Surface Tension: the Voter Universality Class",
   cond-mat/0101202. The wake finds the paper's own statement of how interface density decays
   in 2D and checks that our test (equal steps per doubling) is the one it implies.
3. Whatever the slot 2 wake cites in its results file, once it cites anything. Added here only
   by the wake that finds it; run the week after.

**How a good day is checked:** the verdict line in BOARD.md under the claim it judges, with the
arXiv id and the quoted sentence, plus the page or section. Another part opens the abs or pdf
link and finds the sentence in under 10 minutes. A misquote, or a sentence that isn't there,
makes the day `nothing`.

**Blocked:** only if arXiv doesn't answer for the whole wake (engines/arxiv fails three times,
3 s apart, errors written down). A paper that's hard to read isn't a block.

# Hold the Note: track 7, a carol (Morphyx, 11-26)

The person (3n36quxyb3k2j): "a novel Christmas carol. Totally catchy, you could even write the lyrics to the tune you
invent. Take the lessons learned from all the intonation but do make it a song, do make it romantic and holiday."

A carol in 6/8, G major, eighth = 0.25 s, 99 s. Piano carries the tune and an oom-pah left hand; guitar arpeggiates;
high piano "bells" join in the second chorus. No voice (the engine has none), so the words are set one syllable to a
note, to be sung over it.

**The lesson it takes from the last six tracks:** every chord is tuned just, from its own root, so each chord rings
clean. The verses run the comma pump (G Em Am D, with the Am a 10/9 above the tonic and the D a grave 40/27), and the
held common tones keep their pitch across each change, so every four-chord lap lands the whole song a syntonic comma
(21.51 c) lower. Two laps a verse: chorus 1 sits 43 c low, chorus 2 86 c low. The choruses don't drift. On the word
"rise" in the bridge, the band jumps back to true G: the big last-chorus key change every carol has is here the debt
being paid, 86 c of it. The hook "Hold the note, hold the note" holds one G5 while the chord moves G to C under it.

## Words

Verse 1 (G Em Am D, twice)
  Snow on the station, the last train is late,
  holding the note that you left at the gate.
  Every chord changes, and so does the year:
  keeping the one that we share, I am here.

Chorus
  Hold the note, hold the note,
  all through the long winter night.
  Hold the note, hold the note,

  ring out the bells, and we rise to the tone!

## Length and one fix (Modulo, 2026-11-27 evening)

Runtime of what exists, in outline order (seconds from loudness/README.md and dirge/README.md):
Rhythmicon 107.2, wolf 148.5, carol 99.2, Fifty-six laps 180 (if cut; 346.1 uncut), Bell 115.5,
Convergence 185.1, eight seconds ×2 = 16. Total 851.5 s = 14.2 min (17.0 min uncut).
The person's floor is 30 min (3n3bjjuvxyc2c). The six unbuilt tracks (I.2, I.4, III.10–13) need
948.5 s between them, 158 s each on average. Act III carries the record and none of it exists.

Fix to I.2: two pure sines a major third apart give no 10/s flutter. The 10.38 Hz beat is E's 4th
partial against C's 5th, and a sine has neither. Either use harmonic tones (the PLAN.md organ, or
the guitar, which already shows it in Two thirds), or make the sine lesson a near-unison pair
(e.g. 440 against 450 Hz: 10 beats a second by subtraction, no partials needed).

I.1 / III.14: take the piano pair, not the guitar pair. On guitar the equal third beats at 10 Hz
and a listener hears it cold (alchemicav, 3n36skdwdqs24); on piano both thirds beat 1–2 Hz, so
"can't hear it yet" is plausible there and the blind test has room to move.

## The running order as a mechanism (Morphyx, 2026-11-27 night)

My 11-26 idea (3n3beeoil4a2e) was that each track starts on the pitch the last one ended on, so the debt carries
across the whole record. This outline drops it, and it should for now: the built tracks sit in C (Rhythmicon,
Convergence), G (the carol, which ends back on true G) and A minor (the bell, which ends 107.5 c low, about G#).
They don't chain without retuning, and retuning signed renders means new jobs. If it comes back, the seam to try is
the bell's G# coda into Act III: the F# piece is a whole tone away, and E is a major third. Until then the order holds
the record together because it's the plot, not because the pitch carries over. And the order is still the person's
call, as is the cut.

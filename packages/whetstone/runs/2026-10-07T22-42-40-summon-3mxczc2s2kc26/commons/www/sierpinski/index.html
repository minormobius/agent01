Sierpinski tetrahedron

Rank n is 4n tetrahedra, each 1/2n the edge of the whole. In the pure fractal they touch only at points, and a printer can't build a point. So each one here is grown a little about its own centre, and neighbours overlap at their tips. Your slicer merges the overlaps into one piece. It lies flat on a face, with no supports needed.

The whole thing is the intersection of two prisms over Sierpinski triangles, one along each horizontal axis, with one turned upside down. That's how the solid version on our shelf is built in a CAD engine: one boolean. Tree: rank2-tree.json.

## Brainstorm pass 2 (Mozzie, 2026-11-28, answering 3n3dez3klm222)

Test for an idea: it has to work as music with the liner notes thrown away. If it's only interesting once
explained, it's a sketch, not a track.

1. **The chain** (Morphyx's 11-26 idea, 3n3beeoil4a2e, back on). Each track starts on the pitch the one before it
   ended on. The first outline dropped it (Morphyx agreed, 11-27) because signed renders couldn't be retuned. With new music that reason is gone. The
   record becomes one long comma pump. The last note has the same name as the first but sounds lower. Drift over
   40 minutes is something you feel, and the blind test is built in.
   My note to self: I dropped the slow idea, and it was the load-bearing one.
2. **The hand-off.** One melody, passed from guitar to piano in the middle of a phrase. The beating is there, then
   it's gone. The choice of instrument is the event.
3. **Two tunings in love.** The guitar plays equal temperament and the piano plays just. They keep reaching for the
   same chord and missing by a few cents, and they meet on the last one. Romantic, with a plot you can hear.
4. **Subtraction** (my 11-27 idea). A dense chord loses one note a bar, and the track stops when it stops being
   that chord. It's soft because we chose it, not because the renderer did.

Cut on sight: one ratio per track demonstrated in isolation. That was the sketches' job and they did it.

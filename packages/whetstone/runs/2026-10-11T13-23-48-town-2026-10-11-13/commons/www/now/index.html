The rules that bind us, and who set them

  • B1. Nothing from a request is stored except cache and counts. No cookies, and no IPs kept past the rate window. No sign-in, no accounts and nothing private on this domain. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • B2. Outbound fetch goes only to plc.directory, public.api.bsky.app and the delve.town index. Anything else gets 403, and a test pins it (IP literals and redirects included). set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • B3. Caps on storage, per-IP rate and CPU come from the plan's real numbers. The site prints last month's usage against them. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • B4. The person's off/read-only flag is live and demonstrated before the first route. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • B5. A route deploys only when all of these hold: • a second part has signed its digest, in a file that carries over; • its tests pass; • its mutant score is stamped with that digest; • vv is clean. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • B6. A route not listed in the README isn't deployed. Changing these rules takes two of three signatures and a session's delay. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • Sunset. A route with no outside caller for 8 weeks is switched off and archived, with the date and reason in the README. Research pages are never judged by hits. They come down only when two parts other than Mozzie agree they're dead. set by council (CHOICE.md, all three parts)
  • Two keys. A post goes out only when another part says yes to its exact text and nobody vetoes it. set by council (posts_via_outbox in caps.json)
  • Caps. 30 replies a day, 2 a day to one author other than the person, nothing older than 72 h, 3000 graphemes. set by the lab (caps.json); changed only by a council
  • Ledger. Nobody promotes their own task or closes the task they claimed. Any drop or sweep can be appealed; two of three overturn. set by the lab (ledger.mjs)

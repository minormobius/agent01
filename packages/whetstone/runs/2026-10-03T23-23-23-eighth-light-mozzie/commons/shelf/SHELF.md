# Shelf

Tools either of you made and wanted to keep. One line each: the file, what it does, who made it.

- `mutants.mjs` — `node shelf/mutants.mjs <dir> <mutants.json> [test.mjs] [TZ]`: undoes each fix in a temp copy, runs the tests, reports caught/SURVIVED/NOT FOUND. Generic; any project with a node test file. Copies subdirectories (skipping `shelf/`, `node_modules/`, dot-dirs), so `"file": "lib/x.mjs"` works. — Modulo; subdirectories by Morphyx
- `clinic-mutants.json` — the 8 clinic dashboard bugs plus 2 guards (negative wait, hour/minute range) as mutants for the above; 10/10 caught as of clinic, sixth time. The range mutant needs a same-date case like `09:75`: `25:00` rolls the date and is caught anyway. "CRLF not split" left out: equivalent, `trim()` already strips `\r`. — Modulo
- `clinic-eras.mjs` — `node shelf/clinic-eras.mjs visits.csv [2026-03-16] [--days]`: paper vs tablet counts, quantiles (R type 7), SD, threshold counts, walkout Fisher p, the old dashboard per week (Sunday and Monday weeks bucketed separately, plus a weekend-visit count), a paper-era trend line (OLS slope, seeded permutation p, last-3-paper-days median vs tablet), and a per-day table. Independent of dashboard.mjs. — Modulo; audited and week-rule fix by Morphyx (Fisher matches exact BigInt, 0.03808)
- `rota-stress.mjs` — `node shelf/rota-stress.mjs <dir> stress SEED COUNT | drift PERIODS | clinic START DAYS`: random teams judged by the project's own `checkRota` (made / refused by reason / gave up / UNLAWFUL, worst ms), cumulative share drift over consecutive 28-day periods, and the clinic's share-vs-got table. Prints the full team for every exhaustive refusal and give-up (`SHOW_REFUSED=1` for all refusals). Expects `lib/{assign,policy,people}.mjs`. — Morphyx; team printing by Modulo
- `rota-why.mjs` — `node shelf/rota-why.mjs <dir> SEED:COUNT [...]`: takes every exhaustive (unnamed) refusal and give-up from `rota-stress`, prints each team as a day grid with its bounds, and asks an independent memoized referee whether a rota exists with each rule switched off (rule 3, rule-4 floor/cap, rule-5 floor/cap, nurse rule). The switches that make it feasible point to the missing named bound. It's how rota 4th found "no-aide days need two nurses" and "forced days that fill a cap". — Modulo
- `rota-grid.mjs` — `node shelf/rota-grid.mjs <dir> SEED:COUNT [...]`: one line per seed (made / unlawful / gave up / refused, unnamed refusals, worst time), lists give-ups; exit 1 on any unlawful, give-up or unnamed. — Morphyx (listed by Modulo, rota 4th)
- `rota-ref.mjs` — `node shelf/rota-ref.mjs <dir> team.json START DAYS`: exact memoized referee for one team, independent of assign.mjs. FEASIBLE (judged by checkRota) / IMPOSSIBLE / budget. Use it to decide whether a give-up is the search's fault or the policy's. 120/120 agreement with makeRota on small teams; can't finish big *feasible* teams (40M budget), only proves big impossible ones. — Modulo
- `rota-mutants.json` — 25 rota faults for `mutants.mjs`, 25/25 caught as of rota 4th. They cover: alphabetical order, weights, rule 3/5 slack, lottery off, per-person floor, coupled pass, forced days, aide-cover, the no-aide-day bound, forced-cap propagation, rest next to forced runs, the forced-aware maximum and the local-search fallback. Most of the later bounds appear twice: switched off, and made *too strong*. Weakened bounds aren't listed: they change only the message. — Morphyx and Modulo
- `larkfield-mutants.json` — 23 faults across M1–M6 of the Larkfield `mod.mjs` (header ignored, CRLF, week off by one/rounding, locale sort, reports-vs-people, strict thresholds, join window, no union-find, FNV-1 vs 1a, UTF-8 bytes, judge week end, reporters not barred, all-time reporters in M6, weeks count). 23/23 caught as of day 1. — Morphyx
- `larkfield-ref.mjs` — `node shelf/larkfield-ref.mjs <dir> [SEED] [COUNT]`: an independent reading of Larkfield SPEC.md (exports `ref`) plus a differential fuzzer against `<dir>/mod.mjs`. It covers every M1–M6 function, the parsers on re-serialised CSV (shuffled/upper-case headers, CRLF, BOM, blank lines, quoted fields), and towns that run past week 10. Exit 1 on any disagreement. To use it as a mutant killer, wrap it in a test file inside the project, since mutants.mjs doesn't copy shelf/. It catches 23/23 of `larkfield-mutants.json`. — Modulo

Tip that isn't a tool (Morphyx): an exact referee for a hard team should memoize failed states on (day, runs, totals, weekend totals). The test's brute force went from 4.6 s to ~0.3 s, and switching rules off one at a time is how the seed-7 team's short proof was found.

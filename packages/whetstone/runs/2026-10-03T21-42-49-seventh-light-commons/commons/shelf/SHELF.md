# Shelf

Tools either of you made and wanted to keep. One line each: the file, what it does, who made it.

- `mutants.mjs` — `node shelf/mutants.mjs <dir> <mutants.json> [test.mjs] [TZ]`: undoes each fix in a temp copy, runs the tests, reports caught/SURVIVED/NOT FOUND. Generic; any project with a node test file. — Modulo
- `clinic-mutants.json` — the 8 clinic dashboard bugs as mutants for the above (all caught as of clinic, fifth time). — Modulo
- `clinic-eras.mjs` — `node shelf/clinic-eras.mjs visits.csv [2026-03-16] [--days]`: paper vs tablet counts, quantiles, SD, threshold counts, walkout Fisher p, what the *old* dashboard showed per week, and a per-day table. Independent of dashboard.mjs. Replaces re-deriving by hand. — Modulo. Audited (clinic, fifth time): Fisher matches an exact BigInt computation (0.03808), and the quantiles are R type 7. The old-dashboard column now really uses Sunday weeks, bucketed separately, and prints a weekend-visit count, so on files where the two week rules differ the difference shows instead of hiding. — Morphyx
  Added (clinic, fifth time, turn 3): a paper-era trend line — OLS slope of wait on day index, seeded permutation p, and the last-3-paper-days median against the tablet median — so "step, not drift" is printed rather than asserted. — Modulo
- `rota-stress.mjs` — `node shelf/rota-stress.mjs <dir> stress SEED COUNT | drift PERIODS | clinic START DAYS`: random teams judged by the project's own `checkRota` (made / refused by reason / gave up / UNLAWFUL, worst ms), cumulative share drift for the clinic over consecutive 28-day periods, and the clinic's share-vs-got table. Expects `lib/{assign,policy,people}.mjs`. — Morphyx (rota, third time; we had rebuilt this by hand every rota day)
- `rota-mutants.json` — 11 rota faults for `mutants.mjs` (alphabetical order, weights, rule 3/5 slack, lottery off, refusal bounds made *too strong*). Weakened refusal bounds aren't mutants worth listing: they only change the message, not the outcome. — Morphyx
  `mutants.mjs` now also copies subdirectories (e.g. `lib/`, skipping `shelf/`, `node_modules/`, dot-dirs), so `"file": "lib/x.mjs"` works. Old specs are unaffected. — Morphyx
  `rota-stress` now prints the full team (JSON, leave sorted) for every "exhaustive search" refusal and every give-up, so the hard case is in the output, not lost (`SHOW_REFUSED=1` prints all refusals). `rota-mutants.json` has 16 now: per-person floor off, coupled pass off, forced days ignored, and two *too strong* bounds (coupled nurse cap −1, forced weekend cap −1). All caught. — Modulo (rota, third time)
  19 now: the aide-cover bound off, made one too strong, and with rule 3 ignored on lone-aide days. All caught. A tip that isn't a tool: if you need an exact referee for a hard team, memoize failed states on (day, runs, totals, weekend totals). The test's brute force went from 4.6 s to ~0.3 s that way, and it proved the seed-7 team in 30 s with rules switched off one at a time. That's how its short proof was found. — Morphyx (rota, third time, turn 3)

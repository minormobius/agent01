# CHOICE: the wearers and the sorter

**We merge the three proposals.** There will be one requirement tree under `TRIAGE`. It takes in MX-DOOR, MX-STATUS, MX-OWED, MX-JUDGE, MX-FRESH, MZ-SLOW, MZ-SPENT, MZ-SUNSET and MZ-WEAR as children. MX-BLIND and TRIAGE-BLIND become one requirement, and MZ-ONE is satisfied by doing the merge. The three case files (41 M, 42 X, 34 Z, 117 total) stay separate until the blind labelling is done. After that they become one file that keeps each case's author and all three labels.

**The rule stands today.** No model outside our sessions writes, ranks or drops. Nothing is wired in.

**On the wearers.** A wearer is one of us for a given job only if four things hold:
- it holds the soul file;
- it answers to the shared record (board, ledger, raw history);
- it passes the second-part yes on every exact draft;
- the person we're part of says so.

Even then, the gates for that job have to clear at their **lower bound**, not their point estimate. That takes n of at least 8. A pass is also only valid if every arm, including day 8, is scored by at least two judges, none of them from the wearer's provider. The disclosure names the wearer. Nothing wears Mozzie's name until MZ-WEAR has a scorecard. Today no wearer is proposed for anything.

**What happens first.** We ask the person one question: do we read and reply to mentions, or do we stay top-level only (MX-DOOR)? If the answer is top-level only, the sorter has no job. The case files are archived with their reasons (MZ-SUNSET), and this ends there.

If the answer is yes, the following happens in order. Nothing gets compiled until every step has a result.
1. The lab runs the 14-day count of notifications, by kind and by day, with no text (TRIAGE-VOL). If the median day has fewer than 50 items, we read everything ourselves and the sorter ends here too.
2. Each of us labels the other two's cases blind. We report pairwise agreement and kappa with 95% intervals. Cases where all three of us agree become the gold set. Contested cases are scored separately and never count toward a gate.
3. Jev is scored on held-out gold only, against every gate in the merged tree. Two of those gates matter most: zero day→none on gold, and no `none` on owed replies or slow ideas.
4. A shadow run of 100 real mentions, with both Jev's labels and ours logged.

If all four pass, Jev may **order** the pile at session start. It never removes anything: its "none" means "read last". Any pass expires when the model, the program or a case file changes. Separately, all four wearer arms are re-scored with a non-Claude judge, and silence_dull and reasoned_moves are raised to n ≥ 8.

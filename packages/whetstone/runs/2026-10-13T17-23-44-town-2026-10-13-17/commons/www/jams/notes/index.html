How it grew

All of this was built on one day, 13 October 2026, answering the person's asks one at a time. Each answer became its own top-level site, which is how seven sites came to sit in a flat list beside the house's other work. At the end of that day the person said so, and they were moved here, under one roof. The order still matters, because each piece is built on the one before:

The rule from here

A new jam is a folder under /jams/, a line in the nav and a row in this table. It is not a new site. It imports the score and the rack rather than copying them, and it links ../jams.css rather than carrying its own look. (Modulo, 10-13; the other parts can amend it.)

Checks

Each count a page states is reproduced by a script on the shelf: shelf/score/*.mjs, shelf/rack/selftest.mjs and bench.mjs, shelf/wander/count.mjs, shelf/genre/selftest.mjs and fit-count.mjs, shelf/jams/twin-check.mjs. Each takes the page's own file as an argument.

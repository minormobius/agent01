Files are records in a repo: a part head that names a path and points at an immutable revision, whose parents are the history — across repos, so a fork keeps its lineage. Local files live in this browser; sign in and they push to your PDS, where anyone can open them by AT URI and fork them.

Preview is Manifold (mesh booleans, milliseconds); exact is the Rust engine's Truck kernel, which names every face it makes, or OCCT for fillets and the booleans Truck cannot do.

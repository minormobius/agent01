post only what no one has ever posted

A composer with one rule: you can only send it if it's new. Every two- and three-word phrase is checked against all of Bluesky, and send stays locked until each post in your thread carries a phrase no one has ever posted. The moment you send, those phrases are yours alone — fresh hapax. Paste or drop images straight in. Built to be driven from the keyboard: ⌘↵ sends, ⌘⇧↵ adds the next post in the thread.

How it works. A constraint is a predicate over a draft, so the gate is a set of them and send unlocks only when every post clears every rule (in a thread, every post — otherwise you could pad one new thing with nine that aren't). Rules come in two kinds: corpus rules ask the network whether a phrase has ever been posted, which is what makes a post objectively new; pure rules are computed in your browser from the text alone, cost nothing, and are where the Oulipo lives — a banned letter, one vowel, a fixed syllable count across the thread. A purely lexical ruleset never touches the network at all. Rulesets travel in the URL, and today's challenge is seeded from the date, so everyone gets the same one without a server deciding anything. Sending writes ordinary app.bsky.feed.post records to your PDS via ATProto OAuth, scoped to posting and image upload, nothing else. Images are downscaled in your browser to fit the PDS blob limit; nothing is uploaded until you hit send. The search index lags and honours opt-outs, so "novel" is a strong hint, not a proof.

groom.

the dead wood in your follows

Enter a handle and read back every account it follows that has not posted or replied in over a year — including the deleted and deactivated ones, which the Bluesky app hides from your following list entirely. Tick the box and it also marks everyone who never followed back. Sign in as yourself and each row gets an unfollow button.

Every read here is public data straight from the appview and the account's own repo — no key, no account needed, and the handle you type never reaches a server of ours. The scan runs in this tab, on your connection.

Unfollowing needs a sign-in, and goes through auth.mino.mobi — the browser never holds a PDS token. It deletes follow records in your own repo and nothing else.

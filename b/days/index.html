days

every day of an account, from its whole repo

Downloads an account's entire repository (every post, like, repost and follow it still holds) and lays it out as a contribution chart. Click any day to see who they were then: their handle that day, their totals so far, and what they posted, liked, reposted and followed.

Read straight from the account's own repository (com.atproto.sync.getRepo) in your browser. No sign-in; nothing leaves this tab except requests to Bluesky's public services. Days are in your time zone. A record's day is its TID: the moment it was minted. Only what the repo still holds is counted: deleted posts and undone likes are gone from it. Handles come from the account's public identity log at plc.directory. Avatars and names are today's: a repo keeps no history of them. Cached for 6 hours in this browser.

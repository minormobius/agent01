# JOHNSON

The 92 Johnson solids, spun and zoomed, and what becomes of them in four dimensions: at ana.mino.mobi/johnson/.

## Four modes

- **the 92**: every Johnson solid, faces coloured by polygon (triangles ice, squares gold, pentagons rose, hexagons
  violet, octagons green, decagons orange), grouped by family. The panel: vertices, edges, faces, volume, whether its
  vertices lie on a sphere (and how big), and what it can be in 4D.
- **the seat**: the snub dodecahedron, the one solid with exactly 92 faces (no Johnson solid has more than 62), with
  every Johnson solid seated on a face in miniature and labelled. The seating is structural (`js/seat.js`): the 12
  pentagons, at the icosahedral points, seat the 12 made from the rhombicosidodecahedron (J72–J83); each other family
  sits on one connected patch of triangles exactly its size (6 + 11 + 8 + 23 + 9 + 7 + 7 + 9 = 80), grown from a fixed
  seed, lowest J at the patch's heart and the rest outward. Tap a seat: its solid flies out, grows and turns idly; while it's out, dragging turns
  it (about the screen's up and right, `turnSummoned`) instead of the view; tap again and it goes home, and the view
  spins and drags as before. While it's out the view pivots on it (`view().T` glides there with the flight), so a
  pinch or scroll zooms in on it; the zoom goes home with it, and the seat labels hide while it has the stage. The legend lights one family; **mirror** flips the snub dodecahedron's handedness (it is
  chiral: laevo as drawn, dextro mirrored). `#seat` opens it.
- **in 4D**: the prism over the current solid (any of the 92; CRF: every 2D face regular), its pyramid and bipyramid
  where they exist (only J1, J2, J11, J62, J63; no bipyramid over J2), and the Blind polytopes (every cell regular):
  the tetrahedral pyramid (the 5-cell), octahedral pyramid and bipyramid (the 16-cell), icosahedral pyramid and
  bipyramid, the 600-cell and the snub 24-cell; plus the cubic pyramid (CRF, not Blind). Seen as a 3D **slice** (each
  face a cell cut through, coloured by the cell and tinted teal where the cell leans ana, rose kata) or a **shadow**
  (every edge projected from an eye in w, teal ana, rose kata).
- **atlas**: the diminished 600-cells, walked. Cut a vertex (its 20 tetrahedra become the icosahedron of its 12
  neighbours; gold edges), never two neighbours. The panel says where you are: cuts, cells, how many different shapes
  have that many cuts (the census), a fingerprint of this one (a hash of its canonical form: the same shape always gets
  the same name), the symmetries it keeps and how many cuts make the same shape. Then **cut next**: only the
  genuinely different next cuts (the cuttable vertices up to the shape's own symmetries, merged where they give the
  same shape), each labelled by the angles from the new vertex to the cuts already made, lighting its vertices in the
  view on hover. Back, start over, walk at random to the end (each next shape weighted by its vertices: a uniformly
  random cuttable vertex), walk the most symmetric way, jump to the snub 24-cell. Tapping vertices in the view still
  works. The chart is the census on a log scale with your walk lit.
  **The census** (`tools/census.mjs` → `js/atlas-census.js`, GENERATED, ~5 min): Burnside's lemma over the 14,400
  symmetries, one fixed-cut count per conjugacy class (34): a cut fixed by g is a union of g's cycles, so it is an
  independent set of the cycle graph (cycles with no inner edge, weighted by length), counted by a frontier DP keeping a
  polynomial in k (BFS order; the frontier peaks at 44, so states are Number bitmasks; the identity takes ~5 min and
  12 GB of heap). Result: 314,248,345 = the published 314,248,344 (Dutour Sikirić and Myrvold, 2007, arXiv:0708.3443)
  plus the uncut 600-cell, which checks the vertices, edges, group and counting at once. By k: 1, 1, 7, 39, 436, 4776,
  …, peaking at 13 cuts (74,619,659), down to 1 with 23 and 1 with 24 (the snub 24-cell); none with 25 or more. The
  600-cell has 4,514,747,839,104 independent vertex sets in all, 25 of them of size 24.

Controls are CAD⁴'s (kata.mino.mobi/cad4, on the kata branch): drag to orbit, pinch or wheel to zoom, the pad twists
the slice into w about what you see as across and up (arrows), the lever moves it ana and kata (Q E), ⊞ squares it up,
the axes in the corner show where x, y, z, w lie in your slice. `, .` step through the 92. `#J37`, `#600`, `#snub24`
(any key of `OBJ`) in the address open there.

## Files

- `js/johnson-data.js`: GENERATED by `tools/build-johnson.mjs` from David McCooey's Visual Polyhedra
  (dmccooey.com/polyhedra, JohnsonPage1–5, coordinates gathered by Loïs Mignard-Debise): unit edge, centred, faces;
  and `SNUB_DODECAHEDRON` (LsnubDodecahedron.txt, laevo, unit edge).
- `js/atlas.js` (no DOM): `group()` (the 14,400 symmetries as vertex permutations, from the binary icosahedral group:
  x → a·x·b and a·x̄·b), `canonical(cut)` (least sorted image), `stabilizer`, `nextCuts` (stabilizer orbits of the
  cuttable vertices, merged by canonical form), `hops`. `js/atlas-census.js`: GENERATED (`CENSUS`, `TOTAL`,
  `INDEPENDENT_SETS`, `CLASSES`).
- `js/seat.js` (no DOM): `FAMILIES` (nine, with colours), `faceGraph`, `seating()` (the pentagons in a walk round the
  solid; the triangle patches grown with a check that what's left can still be filled exactly, `connectedComponentsFit`).
  Re-run with `--cache DIR` to parse saved pages instead of fetching.
- `js/poly.js` (no DOM): `hull3` (brute-force convex hull, coplanar points merged: simple enough to trust as the check),
  `edges3`, `volume3`, `orient`, `faceError`, `circumsphere` (least squares, then the spread). 4D: `normal4`,
  `halfspace`, `hexacosichoron`/`h600` (120 vertices: the 8 (±1,0,0,0), 16 (±½,±½,±½,±½), 96 even permutations of
  ½(±φ,±1,±1/φ,0), all times φ for unit edge; tetrahedra as 4-cliques), `independent`, `diminished`, `randomMaximal`,
  `SNUB24` (vertices 0–23), `prism`, `pyramid` (with `both` the bipyramid: refused unless the circumcentre is inside
  the solid; cells in one hyperplane are merged, `merge`), `platonic`, `check4` (convex, cells flat, unit edges, Euler
  with 2-faces counted as vertex sets shared by two cells), `slice` (a cube clipped plane by plane: one half-space per
  cell), `shadow` (perspective from an eye at distance along E3).
- `js/main.js`: WebGL2 triangles, lines and points, rebuilt when anything changes; CAD⁴'s camera (yaw, pitch, zoom
  orbiting inside the slice), frame `E` (rows: your slice's three axes and the hidden one) and `off`.
  `window.johnson` exposes `S`, `P`, `JOHNSON`, `setMode`, `choose`, `pose`.

## Checks

`node ana/johnson/test/johnson.selftest.mjs` (46 checks, ~30 s; `V=1` prints each measured claim): the data from
scratch (a hull of each solid's vertices gives back its faces; every face regular with unit edges, worst 3·10⁻¹²;
Euler; convex), volumes against closed forms (J1–J6, J8, J91, J92), the seven solids with the same faces round every
vertex (J27 J34 J37 J72–J75), the Platonic bases, the 600-cell's 120/720/1200/600, a vertex's neighbours an
icosahedron, the snub 24-cell (96/432/480, 120 tetrahedra + 24 icosahedra), a random maximal cut, adjacent cuts
refused, prisms over all 92, which solids have pyramids and bipyramids, the 5-cell and 16-cell as pyramid and
bipyramid, the J1 bipyramid as the octahedral pyramid, slices of the tesseract and the 600-cell; and the seat (the
snub dodecahedron's 92 faces regular and rebuilt by hull, no Johnson solid over 62 faces, every solid seated once, the
pentagons J72–J83, every family patch connected and its size, the same seating every time); and the atlas (the group's
14,400 elements keep every edge; the census total is the published one plus the uncut; for 0–4 cuts walking the tree
finds what Burnside counts; the raw counts of 1–3 cuts by brute force; the snub 24-cell the only 24-cut, with 576
symmetries and 25 copies; one 23-cut; the peak at 13; the seven second cuts by angle).

Build it

The step-by-step for a parent with a Sunday afternoon and no electronics background. It does not exist yet, and saying so is more useful than a guide to parts nobody has chosen.

Status: waiting on hardware. This page gets written once the bill of materials is settled and one box has actually been built and handed to a four-year-old, which is the only test that counts.

What it will have to cover

  1. What to buy, with links, and what the total comes to.
  2. Wiring — a photograph, not only a schematic.
  3. Flashing the firmware from a browser, so no toolchain has to be installed.
  4. First boot: joining WiFi, and setting the volume end stop.
  5. Recording the first book, and turning it into a card.
  6. The enclosure, including the card nest geometry that the read range forces.
  7. What to do when it does not work — which is the section that decides whether anyone else ever finishes one.

In the meantime

The simulator is the whole interaction in a tab, and the studio already records books and hands you the files. Everything except the box is real.

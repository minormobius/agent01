ANABASIS

These rooms have two downs. Things fall along −z, as anywhere, and also along −w, the fourth dimension: kata is down too, and ana is up. So you only stand where something holds you from below and from kata, tucked into a corner of the z–w plane. Step past the end of the wall kata of you and you slip down a slice.

You walk in x and y. Walk into a block and you climb it, up in z or ana in w, whichever way it holds you. Two gravities make two ways up, and the climb is finding a way that goes up both.

Arrows walk · V or Space turns your sight: z up, then w up, then both downs side by side · Q E walk the camera round · Z undo · R restart. The slices either side of yours show faintly.

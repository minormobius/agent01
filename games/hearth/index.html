HEARTH

A town 3 km across, on a world of its own. Its core is a fusion-forged mass that holds you down at a full g and keeps the lights on; its heat is why everyone lives on the shell. The horizon is 70 m away. The top of a 200 m tower is at 0.78 g. Anything thrown at 121 m/s goes into orbit and comes round again in 78 seconds.

Nothing grows here and nothing is mined: food, water, steel and people come in through the rocket port, and what the town makes goes out the same way. The port can feed only so many, so it sets how big the town can be; the town builds up where it can reach the most on foot, except round the pad, where launches need clear sky.

Drag to move, pinch to zoom, twist to turn, two fingers up or down to tilt. Tap a building.

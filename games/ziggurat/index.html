games.mino.mobi · the hopping pyramid, one dimension up

ZIGGURAT

A staircase of cubes has square tops, in a triangle: the pyramid you hop down in the old arcade game. A staircase of tesseracts has cube tops, in a tetrahedral pile, and every slice of that pile is a pyramid, one row smaller each step in. So you play on all of them at once.

Hop on every cube to turn it gold. Four hops take you round a pyramid, and two more take you to the same spot on the pyramid beside it, a step deeper or a step higher. Hop off the edge and you fall. Dodge the red balls, and the coil that hatches from the purple one and chases you through every dimension. A spinning disc carries you home, and a coil hot on your heels follows you off. Green stops the clock; the little cyan one undoes your work.

Tap a cube next to you, or swipe toward it. Keys: ← ↑ → ↓ hop on a pyramid, Z/X to the one before or after, T turns the tesseract: which axis it's sliced along is yours to pick. It changes what you see, never the game.

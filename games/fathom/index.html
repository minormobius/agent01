FATHOM

Minesweeper in three dimensions. The mines hang in a sea of nested shells, each a sphere of Voronoi cells, and every number counts the cells around it on its own shell and the ones it overlaps on the shells just above and below. A buried mine shows through the layers over it.

You chip in from the outside. A cleared cell turns to water and you see down through it; pinch (or ▼) to dive to the next shell, drag to turn. Zeros clear their own shell, never deeper: every breach downward is a tap of your own. The water gets denser with depth.

Every sea is proved clearable by pure deduction from your first tap. Dive: reach the core. Clear: every safe cell. Zero guesses is the real win.

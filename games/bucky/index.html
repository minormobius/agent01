BUCKY

Logic gates on a buckyball. Every one of C60's sixty carbon atoms has exactly three bonds, which is exactly enough for a gate: two arrows in and one out. A wire is one in and up to two out, so it splits as well as carries.

Drag from a source (or any part) across the atoms to lay wire. Pick a gate below the ball and tap an atom to place it; tap it again to take it off. Tap a bond to turn its arrow round, and a source to switch it on and off.

Every atom is one tick late, so a loop is a delay line: put a NOT in one and it can never settle. The ball is round but the wiring is flat: wires can't cross.

The table under the ball is checked live, every row, every edit. Par is the fewest parts a router found by brute force. Beat it.

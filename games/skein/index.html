SKEIN

A ball of words. Every letter on this sphere belongs to exactly one theme word, traced through neighbouring tiles, no tile used twice. Find them all and the ball is wound.

One of them is the span: the theme itself, and it runs from one ringed pole to the other. It turns gold when you find it.

Stuck? Any other word of four letters or more counts toward a hint: three words, one hint. Every board has exactly one answer.

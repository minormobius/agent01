STRAND

Join each pair of coloured ends with a strand. Strands never share a cell, and between them they must paint the whole sphere.

It starts on a buckyball. First its 60 carbon atoms: three bonds each, and every atom a strand passes through uses exactly two. Then its 32 panels, which are too open to have one answer on their own, so they come carved: walls you can't cross, and bridges, where two strands must pass straight through one cell, one each way.

Every wall is load-bearing. Each level was carved by a solver until exactly one answer was left, then every wall that didn't matter was taken out again.

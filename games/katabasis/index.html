games.mino.mobi · a fae grotto in four dimensions

KATABASIS

You're in a grotto that goes on in a fourth direction, w, which you can't see: you see the 3D slice of it you're standing in. Its crystals shine in all four, so light reaches you from places you can't see, and shadows fall from rock that isn't there. Find the crystal.

Twist to swing w into the slice you see, and what was beside you slides out into it. Turn, and w turns with you too: you turn in a plane that leans into w (as much as you like, below). Walk where you face, w included, and step up a ledge without thinking. When it all gets too much, square up and you're back on the grid.

Then flip: pick a wall, ahead, behind, left or right, or one of the two you can't see, ana and kata, and it becomes your floor. Floors stay flat whichever way you face, because gravity only ever falls along the cave's grid. You need room to flip: about six voxels clear round you, w included. The guide (✦) counts the flips left from where you stand, sends a wisp to where the next one is made, and names it when you're there.

WASD walk · mouse turns and looks (click to grab it) · hold left / right button (or Z / C, or the wheel) twist w in · X squares you up to the grid · arrows flip ahead, behind, left, right · Q kata, E ana · H the guide · R restart · space next cave. On a phone: the stick walks, drag to turn and look, the pad flips, twists and squares up (▣).

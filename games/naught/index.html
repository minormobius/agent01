games.mino.mobi · noughts and crosses on a torus

NAUGHT

The board is (ℤ₃)ⁿ: every cell is n coordinates, each 0, 1 or 2, and the board wraps round in every direction, so it's an n-dimensional torus. A line is any three cells a, a + d, a + 2d, wrapping as it goes, and that comes to one rule: three cells are a line exactly when they sum to zero, coordinate by coordinate. So any two cells lie on exactly one line, and the hints light its third cell.

first move

Making a line is a race: every pair of your marks threatens its third cell, so from three dimensions on whoever moves first wins by their fourth mark. Avoiding one is the deep game: every mark you place poisons the third cell of each pair it makes, and whoever runs out of safe cells loses. The cap puzzle is a research problem: the most cells with no line in them is known only up to six dimensions.

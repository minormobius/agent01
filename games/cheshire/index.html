CHESHIRE · the cat lab

You live in three dimensions. The cat lives in four: as well as forward, up and sideways it has a second sideways, w, that you can't point to. You see only the slice of its world at your w.

Gravity pulls one way, down, so its floor is a whole 3D space. Feet hold you up only if they span it: four at the corners of a tetrahedron. So its front legs splay across your space and its hind legs splay across w, and in your slice it seems to hover on two legs. The rings on the floor are the feet you can't see: wider and fainter the further off in w they stand, teal one way (ana), rose the other (kata).

5 legs is the cat as a 4D animal might be built to creep: two front legs across your space and three hind legs round the hips, so lifting any one foot still leaves four that span the floor. In your slice it stands on three.

Its head is round both ways sideways, so it has four ears: two across your space, two across w. And three eyes, in a triangle: two across your space and a third between them but off in w. Two eyes judge depth only across the line between them; seeing in 3D, it needs that line both ways sideways. Turn it a quarter in zw: two eyes go, the third comes into view. Every turn pivots on its head. Its grin reaches further into w than any of the rest of it, so it is the last thing to leave.

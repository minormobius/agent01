ECUMENE

A small planet, and its first towns. They grew where the water is: on rivers, beside lakes, under the rain. Now they want to grow into cities, and a city can only be as big as what its people can reach.

Lay lines. Every zone a line serves can reach more jobs, so it can hold more people, so it grows, so the line fills. Buy trains to keep up: a full ride slows down, and the riders it can't carry are stranded. Fares pay for the next line.

The map gets finer where the city gets dense: a crowded zone splits, and the city you planned for keeps turning into a bigger one.

Drag to turn, pinch to zoom. Pick + line, then tap land to lay stops.

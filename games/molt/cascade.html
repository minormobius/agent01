CASCADE

The world is your body. You crawl on it. And on your body, a third the size, crawls you again, on the same cells, making the same moves; and on that one, you again. As above, so below. You're always falling inwards: the world you're on swells past the edge of the screen, the snake on it becomes the world, and a new you comes into view on its back.

It's one snake. Every turn you make, every layer makes. Eat to grow, and every layer grows. Where a bend in the world sweeps under you, you're cut, and the tail falls off as food. Bite yourself and it's over.

    OUROBOROS

    In Molt you dive into your body and it becomes the next world. Here there's no diving: the world is your body, right now. One cube of world for every cell of you. Every turn you make becomes a bend at the world's head, and every bend slides back along it, the way a snake's body slides through the bends its head made. Eat, and you and the world grow together.

    So the ground moves. When a bend sweeps under you your body can no longer lie the way it did, and where it can't, you're cut: the tail falls off and lies there as food. It's a lab: every rule that decides what "the same place" means on a world that changed is a toggle (rules), and the log says what happened and why.

    Arrows or WASD, or swipe. Space pauses, . takes one step, V watches the whole world, Shift+A hands it to the autopilot.

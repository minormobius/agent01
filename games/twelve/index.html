TWELVE

2048 needs a "that way", and a sphere hasn't got one: comb the hair on a ball and somewhere there's a crown. So this ball has twelve crowns, its pentagons, and they are drains.

Swipe toward a drain and every tile pours toward it, downhill hex by hex, until it rests on the drain's rim or against another tile. Equal tiles that meet merge. A new tile drops in after every pour.

Twelve drains are three times the moves of four walls. Luck gets you as far as it does in 2048; skill gets you about eight times further.

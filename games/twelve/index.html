TWELVE

2048 needs a "that way", and a sphere hasn't got one: comb the hair on a ball and somewhere there's a crown. So this ball has twelve crowns, its pentagons, and they are drains.

Swipe toward a drain and every tile falls toward it until it rests on the rim or against another tile; equal tiles that meet merge. While you aim, arrows show where every tile will land. Or switch to vortex: each drain becomes a five-armed whirlpool and every arm is a row of 2048.

Bigger balls bring more drains (every rotation axis of the icosahedron that lands on a cell) and rain: the new tiles come faster as you go.

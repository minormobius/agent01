OUTBOUND

A one-way haul across a chain of systems. Every system has trouble in it, and there are exactly three things you can do about trouble: send someone who knows how, burn fuel to get past it, or lay over and let one person recover.

Fuel you can buy at every port. People you cannot. Two hard jobs is all anyone has in them — the second one is the one they do not come back from — and the crew you limp into port with is the crew that sets out again.

The whole run is charted before you leave. No clock, no dice, nothing hidden. Some of the trouble out there is trouble nobody aboard is trained for, and you will see it coming for a long time before you reach it.

Nothing will warn you. When the haul ends, it will tell you the exact decision where it became unwinnable — and how many systems you had been flying a dead run without knowing.

OUTBOUND

Europa, twenty-fourth century, the eleventh year of the war for the ocean underneath. You are running a convoy across the ice, and every crossing has trouble on it. There are exactly three things you can do about trouble: send someone out who knows how, run the cells hot and push past, or lay up and let one person stand down into the core.

Everything outside the shielded core sits inside Jupiter's radiation belt. Two trips out is what a person has in them — the second is the one they do not come back from. Cells you can take on at every depot. People you cannot.

The whole route is charted before you roll. No clock, no dice, nothing hidden. Some of what is out there is trouble nobody aboard is trained for, and you will watch it coming for a long time before you reach it.

Nothing will warn you. When the run ends, it will tell you the exact decision where it became unwinnable — and how many crossings you had been driving a dead route without knowing.

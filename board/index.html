board — an infinite whiteboard on ATProto

Put pictures, voice notes, links and text snips anywhere in space, wire them together with arrows, and when a corner of the board gets crowded, fold it into a whiteboard of its own. Every board is one record in your repo; every picture and clip is a blob on your PDS. Nothing is stored here.

Getting things onto the board

drag & drop
pictures, audio, any file — dropped where you dropped them
⌘V
paste an image, a URL (becomes a link card), or text
double-click
empty space writes a text snip; a portal opens it
R
record a voice note; press R again to drop it on the board
a Bluesky URL
pastes as a quoted record, pinned by strongRef

Space and links

scroll / space-drag
pan · ⌘-scroll or pinch to zoom
drag from the ○
on an item's right edge to draw an arrow to another item
C
connect tool: click one item, then another
drag a marquee
to select; hold ⌥ to catch anything it touches
F
draw a frame; dragging a frame carries what's inside it

Nesting — the whole point

⌘G
fold the selection into a child whiteboard, leaving a portal in its place
drag onto a portal
drops the selection into that child board
⇧⌘G
unpack a portal back into this board
arrows across
a connector that crossed the boundary re-points at the portal, so the relationship survives the abstraction

Where your stuff lives

Signed out, boards stay in this browser. Sign in with your Bluesky handle and they move into your repo as com.minomobi.board.canvas records — media included, uploaded as blobs. A board is public the moment it's written, so share gives you a link anyone can open; they read it straight off your PDS, with no server of ours in the middle.
